# State of the research program

<p class="dek">The counterexample is settled; the surrounding classification, minimality, moduli, homogeneous reduction, and plane-boundary questions are not.</p>

The current candidate incorporates 102 grouped result/problem packages and 355 stable-tag atomic claims. Eight packages are new in this release. Manuscript coverage and independent verification are displayed separately from mathematical status.

## Six programs

<div class="record-grid" markdown>

### [1. Cubic Marked-Root Incidence Geometry](programs/cubic-marked-root-incidence-geometry.md)

**The counterexample loses sheets; how much of its geometry can be classified?**

A program about normalized marked-root constructions, cubic covers, lost sheets, moving hyperplanes, and the boundary between a universal mechanism and features special to the known example.

*18 packages · 3 open*

### [2. Minimum Degree and Quartic Exclusions](programs/minimum-degree-and-quartic-exclusions.md)

**The counterexample has degree seven; how small can a counterexample be?**

A collision-normalized search and a sequence of geometric exclusions isolate what a hypothetical quartic counterexample would have to look like.

*16 packages · 4 open*

### [3. Local Rigidity and Deformation Algebra](programs/local-rigidity-and-deformation-algebra.md)

**The counterexample sits in a large formal deformation space; which deformations survive polynomial equivalence?**

Formal right-triviality, weighted residual directions, and a finite local Artin algebra turn a global equivalence problem into exact local algebra.

*6 packages · 1 open*

### [4. Stable Moduli](programs/stable-moduli.md)

**The counterexample deforms; when do those deformations remain genuinely different after stabilization?**

Several explicit families test how fixed-degree, affine, and stable equivalence interact, with boundary geometry providing computable invariants.

*14 packages · 2 open*

### [5. Homogeneous Descendants](programs/homogeneous-descendants.md)

**The counterexample is not cubic-homogeneous; how efficiently can it be converted into one that is?**

Rank-sensitive suspensions, pairing constructions, sparse obstructions, and low-dimensional Jordan forms organize the search for smaller homogeneous descendants.

*20 packages · 5 open*

### [6. Plane Boundary Obstructions](programs/plane-boundary-obstructions.md)

**The three-dimensional mechanism does not descend directly; what exactly blocks a plane analogue?**

Puiseux expansions, logarithmic geometry, Newton faces, dessins, and terminal boundary operators provide complementary constraints on the still-open plane case.

*17 packages · 7 open*

</div>

## New in this candidate

- [Resolvent Restrictions on a Cubic Normalization Defect](../collections/cubic-resolvent-defect-exclusions.md) — Divisorial sheet loss has only three allowed types, and the quadratic resolvent rules out isolated support for a nonflat cubic defect; a smooth-axis exclusion is conditional on extension of the transverse three-torsion class.
- [The Quartic Target-Span-Two Ramification Filtration](../collections/quartic-target-span-two-ramification-filtration.md) — The quartic search is reduced to target span two. Regular, genuinely nonbinary quadratic-source, simple-ramification, and double-ramification strata are excluded under the manuscript's stated structural inputs; the triple branch is only partially reduced.
- [The Triple-Ramification and Fixed-Component Endgame](../collections/triple-ramification-and-fixed-component-endgame.md) — The generic first obstruction is now certified exactly, but exceptional rank-drop and resonant divisors, the later F4 compatibility check, higher ramification, and fixed-component boundaries remain open.
- [Reconstructing the Kuranishi Ideal from Marked-Root Flows](../collections/marked-root-source-flow-reconstruction.md) — An independently constructed weighted-divergence and Rees complex agrees with the direct determinant complex through quartic order and reproduces the recorded Hilbert prefix and filtered generator counts.
- [The Categorical Quotient of Bounded Cubic Frames](../collections/categorical-cubic-frame-quotient.md) — A differential-kernel model computes the invariant algebra, one root-escape wall controls global invariants, and a weighted graph closure supplies a separated replacement where the fppf orbit quotient is not algebraic.
- [Regular Full-Kernel Pencils as Binary Sextics](../collections/full-kernel-regular-pencil-geometry.md) — The regular five-dimensional full-kernel chart is organized by a scalar and a binary sextic. Thirty exact finite-field samples supply first-normal obstruction evidence, not a characteristic-zero classification.
- [Why the Nineteen-Variable Descendant Resists Compression](../collections/nineteen-to-eighteen-compression-obstructions.md) — After separating row-killing automorphism directions, a six-dimensional exact rank-six cone remains; every point integrates through cubic order, while a universal quartic cokernel functional blocks the entire transversal.
- [A Stored Degree-21 Terminal Model Does Not Glue](../collections/stored-degree-twenty-one-terminal-no-gluing.md) — A missing layer-four operation is identified as a Newton-chart transition. In the stored specialization, the resulting layer-five-through-seven support and matching equations have no solution over the algebraic closure of the coefficient field.
