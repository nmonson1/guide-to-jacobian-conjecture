## The central idea

The theorem-level package is centered on the following mechanism: For a quartic Keller map with leading line H4=(P,Q,0), the first nonzero normal layer Nm is algebraic but not rational over C(P,Q), and the characteristic-field extension has degree at least 4/gcd(4,m).  Its supporting records isolate the ingredients that establish the statement and the qualifications that control its scope.

## Precise statement

In the primitive coprime quartic leading-line case, the surviving normal-degree-three valuation pattern is uniquely the partition 2+1, reducing the leading data to H4=(ell^4,q2^2,0) and (H3)3=ell q2 with ell not dividing q2.
