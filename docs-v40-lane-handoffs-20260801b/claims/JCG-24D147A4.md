# The Relative Jacobian Blowup Recovers the Finite-Root Chart

<p class="dek">The delta_a chart of the blowup of the relative Jacobian ideal (delta_a,delta_b) is exactly A2 with coordinates (c,t), and the pulled-back ideal is generated by rho(c)H0(c,t)^3.</p>

## Exact statement

The delta_a chart of the blowup of the relative Jacobian ideal (delta_a,delta_b) is exactly A2 with coordinates (c,t), and the pulled-back ideal is generated by rho(c)H0(c,t)^3.
