\[
S(X,w)=(X,w-q(X)),\qquad
T(U,V)=(U+BV,V).
\]
Both are triangular polynomial automorphisms, and direct substitution gives
\[
\widetilde K=T\circ(K\times\id_{\A^r})\circ S.
\]
