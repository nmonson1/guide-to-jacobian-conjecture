## The central idea

The theorem-level package is centered on the following mechanism: Let \((A,B)\) and \((\widetilde A,\widetilde B)\) be admissible cubic frames with \(A/c\) and \(\widetilde A/c\) nonconstant. Stable left–right equivalence, ordinary polynomial left–right equivalence, and the existence of \(u\in\mathbf C^\times\) such that \(\widetilde A(uc)=uA(c)\) and \(\widetilde B(uc)-B(c)\in cA(c)\mathbf C[c]\) are equivalent. No squarefreeness or coprimality hypothesis is required.  Its supporting records isolate the ingredients that establish the statement and the qualifications that control its scope.
