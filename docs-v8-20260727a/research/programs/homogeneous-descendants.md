# Homogeneous Descendants

<p class="dek">The counterexample is not cubic-homogeneous; how efficiently can it be converted into one that is?</p>

<span class="status status-draft">Working research program</span>

## The mathematical idea

Many classical reductions replace a general Keller map by a cubic-homogeneous one in more variables.  After a counterexample exists, the natural quantitative question is how economically that replacement can be performed.

## For a first reading

This program starts from a public degree-at-most-three descendant and measures the span of its cubic coordinates.  That rank, rather than the raw number of monomials, controls a collision-preserving suspension and the size of the resulting homogeneous map.

## The proof strategy

The same tensor is then studied through nilpotent Jordan type, Hessian constructions, square-zero pairings, Waring bounds, and equivariant compression obstructions.  A companion development gives the exact five-dimensional collision chart and asks whether its first-normal cokernel obstruction is globally nonzero.  A second development asks how collision minimality produces a monolith whose multiplication algebra lies in a special-linear or symplectic prolongation.

## Scope and current boundary

The manuscript credits the classical homogeneous reductions and the public low-dimensional inputs.  Its numerical bounds are for the displayed construction or stated symmetry class, not universal minimality theorems.  In dimension five, the fifteen chart quintics and all seventeen sampled rank profiles are exact, but global saturation and nowhere-solvability of the cokernel obstruction remain open.  The monolith extension problem E(N) is also open.

## Results in this program

### [A 38-Dimensional Hessian Quartic from Five Reduction Strategies](../../results/hessian-quartic-five-attacks.md)

The five-attacks package constructs a 38D Hessian quartic with Hessian Jordan type (35,2,1) and derives exact central-binomial inverse-ray coefficients with infinitely many nonzero Laplacian terms in the stated parity class.

### [A Cubic-Jet Rank Obstruction to Further Homogeneous Descent](../../results/cubic-jet-rank-obstruction.md)

The unique scalar-monomial quadratic shift lowering the cubic jet from rank seven to six is P2=-d^2 e_a, and its quartic error is detected by an exact 13-term functional outside the cubic homological image; the natural quadratic target cleanup also fails.

### [Bounds and Obstructions for Symmetric Dilations](../../results/symmetric-dilation-bounds.md)

If an n-dimensional homogeneous map has a k-dimensional partial-gradient block, it admits a weak symmetric dilation in dimension at most 2n-k; in particular every polynomial map has weak symmetric-dilation dimension at most 2n-1.

### [Collision Monoliths and Prolongation](../../results/collision-monolith-and-prolongation.md)

A dimension-minimal cubic-homogeneous counterexample cannot have an arbitrary collision algebra: its collision generates a large monolith governed by special-linear or symplectic prolongation.

### [Cubic Homogeneous Descendant Construction](../../results/cubic-homogeneous-descendant-construction.md)

The explicit constructions give the upper bound N_min <= 19 for cubic-homogeneous counterexamples; the conversation uses the elementary no-collinear-collision argument for the lower bound N_min >= 5.

### [Drużkowski Pairing](../../results/druzkowski-pairing.md)

The explicit pairing dictionary gives a 110D Druzkowski construction, while the derivative-space zero-block and equality-case argument give the model-relative bound 52 <= pairing rank <= 110 for the fixed 19D tensor.

### [Filtered Regular Nilpotent Pencils in Dimension Five](../../results/filtered-regular-pencils-in-dimension-five.md)

An everywhere-regular five-dimensional quadratic nilpotent line pencil whose kernel map spans P4 has saturated filtration degrees (-4,-2,0,2,4); every successive map is an isomorphism and the kernel map is the rational normal quartic.

### [Mathieu Correction And Valid Consequence](../../results/mathieu-correction-and-valid-consequence.md)

The new 24-variable map improves this to \(\mathrm{MC}(SU(N))\) false for all \(N\ge24\).

### [Second Four Dimensional Counterexample](../../results/second-four-dimensional-counterexample.md)

A second counterexample \(G:\mathbb C^4\to\mathbb C^4\) has determinant \(4\) and generic degree \(8\).

### [Sparse Exclusions in Dimension Five](../../results/dimension-five-partial-classification.md)

In the five-dimensional square-zero two-matrix ansatz with a coordinate collision, exactly three active rows, one directed support edge in each active row, and all five vertices used by the three edges, the Keller equations have no solution.

### [The Common Cover of the Descendant Ladder](../../results/common-cover-of-the-descendant-ladder.md)

The original three-variable map has the displayed invariant quotient cubic theta^3-2theta^2+Btheta-2A, whose irreducibility and nonsquare discriminant give generic Galois group S3; the descendant ladder presents this same three-sheeted cover.

### [The Explicit Eleven-Dimensional Cubic Counterexample](../../results/eleven-dimensional-cubic-counterexample.md)

The cited map from complex affine 11-space to itself has total degree 3, 52 nonzero monomial terms, constant Jacobian determinant -2, and three displayed distinct rational inputs with one common image.

### [The Regular-Jordan Collision Family in Dimension Five](../../results/dimension-five-regular-jordan-family.md)

The everywhere-regular Jordan-type (5) collision-line equations in dimension five have a smooth one-dimensional characteristic-zero family, obtained from an explicit smooth F11 point and Hensel lift.

## Open problems in this program

### [Can the Dimension-Five First-Normal Obstruction Be Globalized?](../../results/global-first-normal-obstruction-in-dimension-five.md)

Prove that the first-normal obstruction covariant Omega is nowhere zero on the full-kernel regular collision-line curve, thereby excluding the entire regular Jordan-type-(5) line stratum rather than one residue disk.

### [Can the Symmetric Descendant Be Made Smaller and Keller?](../../results/smaller-keller-symmetric-dilations.md)

The direct Euclidean 36-dimensional partial-gradient potential is not Keller when B is nonzero; obtaining a 36-dimensional Keller or cover-preserving realization is a separate self-adjoint nilpotent matrix-completion problem.
