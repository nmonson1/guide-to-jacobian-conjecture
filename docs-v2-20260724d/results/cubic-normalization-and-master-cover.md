## The central idea

The theorem-level package is centered on the following mechanism: Every generic-degree-three Keller map factors through a finite normal cubic cover whose trace-zero module is rank-two reflexive and locally free away from finitely many target points.  Its supporting records isolate the ingredients that establish the statement and the qualifications that control its scope.
