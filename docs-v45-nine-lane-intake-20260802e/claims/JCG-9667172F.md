## Current replacement

In lower-face coordinates t=Y, z=XY^2, the wall shear Y'=Y+lambda X^(-k) has t'=t(1+h), z'=z(1+h)^2 with h=lambda t^(2k-1)z^(-k), so its infinitesimal normal order is r=2k-1. For k=4 the bare wall therefore begins at layer seven, not four, and no filtration-preserving conjugacy with invertible associated graded lowers it to layer four. Through layer 15, the exact density transports form an additive finite wall groupoid with nonlinear master-equation covariance, inverse and cocycle laws, contragredient residue-dual transport, and explicit pairwise/triple intersections. The corrected associated-graded layer-four candidate is a Hamiltonian flow that linearizes as Q -> Q+16s on a degree-eight monomial quotient, but its identification with the archived layer-four residual and the intended adjacent complete-chain chart remains open.
