# The Bounded Cubic-Frame fppf Orbit Quotient Is Not Algebraic

<p class="dek">Let R be a DVR with uniformizer pi and let e >= 1. The rank-one wall orbit sheaf B/Ann_B(pi^e) fails the strong Rim-Schlessinger pushout condition, so it is not an algebraic space; consequently the free quotient stack and the bounded cubic-frame fppf quotient along the generic one-root wall are not algebraic. At e = 0 the acting group K_0 is trivial and the quotient is algebraic.</p>

## Exact statement

Let R be a DVR with uniformizer pi and let e >= 1. The rank-one wall orbit sheaf B/Ann_B(pi^e) fails the strong Rim-Schlessinger pushout condition, so it is not an algebraic space; consequently the free quotient stack and the bounded cubic-frame fppf quotient along the generic one-root wall are not algebraic. At e = 0 the acting group K_0 is trivial and the quotient is algebraic.
