# The Remaining Leading-Line Geometry in Degree Four

<p class="dek">In the primitive coprime quartic leading-line case, the surviving normal-degree-three valuation pattern is uniquely the partition 2+1, reducing the leading data to H4=(ell^4,q2^2,0) and (H3)3=ell q2 with ell not dividing q2.</p>

## Precise statement

In the primitive coprime quartic leading-line case, the surviving normal-degree-three valuation pattern is uniquely the partition 2+1, reducing the leading data to H4=(ell^4,q2^2,0) and (H3)3=ell q2 with ell not dividing q2.
