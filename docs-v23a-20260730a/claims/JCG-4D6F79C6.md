# Low Radial Different Forces a Rational Marked Root

<p class="dek">Let L/K(lambda) be the cubic radial extension at the exceptional marked-root end, unramified over lambda=0 with residue degree three and residue field K*. If the geometric radial cover has different degree less than four, or if all geometric inertia groups fix a common sheet, then it is geometrically disconnected and L=K*(lambda); equivalently, the direction map has a rational marked-root lift.</p>

## Exact statement

Let L/K(lambda) be the cubic radial extension at the exceptional marked-root end, unramified over lambda=0 with residue degree three and residue field K*. If the geometric radial cover has different degree less than four, or if all geometric inertia groups fix a common sheet, then it is geometrically disconnected and L=K*(lambda); equivalently, the direction map has a rational marked-root lift.
