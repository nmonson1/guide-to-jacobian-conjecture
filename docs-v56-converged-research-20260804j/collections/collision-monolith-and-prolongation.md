# Collision Monoliths and Prolongation

<p class="dek">A dimension-minimal cubic-homogeneous counterexample cannot have an arbitrary collision algebra: its collision generates a large monolith governed by special-linear or symplectic prolongation.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

For a dimension-minimal noninjective cubic-homogeneous Keller map, the collision algebra has a unique minimal ideal \(D\) containing the collision direction, with \(\dim D\ge\lceil(n-1)/2\rceil\). Over an algebraic closure, the Lie algebra generated on \(D\) by partial multiplications is \(\mathfrak{sl}(D)\) or \(\mathfrak{sp}(D,\omega)\).
