# The F2 Terminal Boundary and Its Local Flexibility

<p class="dek">For every m at least two in the F2 complete-chain family, the final regular corner has a unique normalized face polynomial q_m and a unique degree-2m Belyi boundary map with passport (m,m), (2m-1,1), (3,1^(2m-3)) and monodromy group A_{2m}.</p>

## Precise statement

For every m at least two in the F2 complete-chain family, the final regular corner has a unique normalized face polynomial q_m and a unique degree-2m Belyi boundary map with passport (m,m), (2m-1,1), (3,1^(2m-3)) and monodromy group A_{2m}.

The local F2 defect recurrence is integrable: for every m at least two it has an explicit nontrivial finite-support solution, so no obstruction based only on this local finite-order recurrence can eliminate the family.

*Defining · Supporting Result · Recorded*

### [JCG-9369706B · For the infinite F2 complete-chain family, the first face has exactly two…](../claims/JCG-9369706B.md)

For the infinite F2 complete-chain family, the first face has exactly two normalized solutions with rigid degree-three and degree-six dessins, and the exact-power lift P=S^m is impossible for every m>=2 by an odd-versus-even divisor-valuation obstruction.

*Shared · Supporting Result · Recorded*

### [JCG-2533E53C · The apparent F2 external conditions at orders 510 and 520 are slice-dependent…](../claims/JCG-2533E53C.md)

The apparent F2 external conditions at orders 510 and 520 are slice-dependent and can both be cancelled exactly over Q by later kernel parameters; neither is a gauge-invariant terminal obstruction.
