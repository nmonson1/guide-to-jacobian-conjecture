# No plane Keller counterexample below maximum coordinate degree 125

`RMU-6D8E0014` · `corollary` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

There is no characteristic-zero plane Keller counterexample (P,Q) with max(deg P,deg Q)<125.

## Hypotheses

- The base field has characteristic zero and [P,Q] is a nonzero constant.
- The published GGHV Theorem 2.1, Proposition 4.1, Proposition 4.3, and Corollary 5.7 are used with their stated hypotheses.
- The internal five-face classification, direct two-support closure, and compact toric terminal theorem are used exactly as cited.

## Applies to

- Every characteristic-zero plane polynomial Keller map of maximum coordinate degree strictly less than 125.

## Limitations

- No novelty or priority claim is made for the degree-125 bound.
- The literature reduction is source-checked and cited, not independently reproved in this repository.
- The conclusion is a finite degree bound, not the plane Jacobian conjecture.

## Arguments

### Assembly of the characteristic-zero below-125 exclusion

`ARG-L8-BELOW125-ASSEMBLY` · `reduction`

The cited GGHV reduction leaves the normalized (8,28) supports after excluding (9,27), and the direct two-root theorem closes both remaining cases.

Apply the cited GGHV degree inventory and complete-chain reductions with their published hypotheses. Their results exclude the (9,27) case and route the remaining (8,28) case to the two normalized supports. Invoke the internal five-face classification, compact toric terminal theorem, and ARG-L8-DIRECT-TWO-ROOT-CLOSURE to exclude both supports. Therefore no characteristic-zero plane Keller counterexample has maximum coordinate degree strictly below 125. This is a proof assembly with an imported reduction and makes no priority claim.
