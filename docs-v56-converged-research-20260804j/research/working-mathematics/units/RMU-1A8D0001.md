# Canonical finite Ext defect for a cubic Keller normalization

`RMU-1A8D0001` · `proposition` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

Let R=C[y_1,y_2,y_3], let B be the finite normalization attached to a generic-degree-three Keller map, and write B=R direct-sum E by trace. Then B and E are reflexive and Delta_F=Ext^1_R(B,R)=Ext^1_R(E,R) has finite length. Its support is exactly the locus where B is not free over R. Consequently B is finite flat over R if and only if Delta_F=0. At a closed point y, a minimal resolution 0 -> A^b -> A^(b+2) -> E_y -> 0 over A=R_y presents (Delta_F)_y as the cokernel of the dual matrix, with b minimal generators.

## Hypotheses

- The ground field is C.
- F:A^3->A^3 is a Keller map with separable generic degree three.
- B is the integral closure of the target coordinate ring in the source function field.

## Applies to

- The intrinsic finite flatness defect of every generic-degree-three complex Keller normalization.

## Limitations

- The proposition identifies and localizes the defect but does not prove that it vanishes.

## Arguments

### The canonical Ext defect and its completed local consequences

`ARG-P1-CANONICAL-EXT-DEFECT` · `proof`

Reflexivity and a length-one local resolution identify the exact nonfree-locus defect; the oriented rank-two summand makes its resolution alternating, and completed factor analysis gives the unique defective branch and fibre length.

Split the finite normalization by trace as B=R direct-sum E. Since B is normal and finite over the regular threefold R, both B and the rank-two trace-zero summand E are reflexive. At a closed point y, E_y has depth at least two, so Auslander--Buchsbaum gives a minimal resolution 0 -> A^b -> A^(b+2) -> E_y -> 0 over A=R_y. Dualizing shows that Ext^1_A(E_y,A) is the cokernel of the dual matrix; minimality makes it nonzero exactly when b>0. Thus Delta_F=Ext^1_R(E,R) has finite length and support precisely the nonfree locus, and Delta_F=0 is equivalent to finite flatness. The determinant trivialization of E gives a perfect alternating pairing on the punctured spectrum. Reflexive extension and comparison with the minimal resolution make the presentation alternating and identify Delta_F with its Matlis dual; when b=1 the three matrix entries form a regular sequence and the defect is their complete-intersection quotient. Finally, complete B at a defect point and decompose it into complete local normal factors. Rank-one and rank-two factors are free, while a cyclic rank-three factor is free by character decomposition. Hence precisely one non-Galois rank-three factor can carry the defect. Its special fibre is supported at one point, and the minimal presentation gives fibre length b+3.

Does not establish:

- That the Ext defect vanishes for every cubic Keller map.
- Existence or nonexistence of a defective completed branch.
