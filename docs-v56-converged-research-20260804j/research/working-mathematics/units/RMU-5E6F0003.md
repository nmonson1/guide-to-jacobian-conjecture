# Degree-six mixed-return family retains only constants

`RMU-5E6F0003` · `computational_result` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

Let S=k[P,Q,R] be the displayed Lane 5 image algebra over a characteristic-zero field and let Psi_{N,M}(x,y,z)=(x,y+x^N,z+(y+x^N)^M). For every 2<=N,M<=6, Psi_{N,M}(S) intersect k[x,y,z]_{<=6}=k. Equivalently F_6^{Psi_{N,M}}A=k, the generated algebra has transcendence degree zero, and J_6^{Psi_{N,M}}=0.

## Hypotheses

- The embedded image algebra S=k[P,Q,R] and source-composition convention are exactly those displayed in the certificate packet.
- The coefficient field has characteristic zero.
- The exponents satisfy 2<=N,M<=6.

## Applies to

- All 25 ordered mixed-return pairs in the displayed bounded family.

## Limitations

- The theorem does not classify exponents outside the bounded range, every resonant mixed-sign tame word, or wild polynomial automorphisms.
- The finite table is not a general transformation law for the degree filtration.
