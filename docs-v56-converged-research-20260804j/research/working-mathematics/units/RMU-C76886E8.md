# Rational Betti certification

`RMU-C76886E8` · `question` · statement version `2`

## Exact statement

Produce exact rational rank or homology certificates for beta_3 through beta_9. The border presentation reduces this to a finite weight-by-weight calculation independent of the original Kuranishi elimination; beta_2=354 over Q is already certified by RMU-3B2E0001.

## Hypotheses

- The transverse local algebra and border presentation are the fixed objects in the rational-Betti packet.
- Characteristic-zero conclusions require exact rational ranks or homology certificates; good-prime agreement is only a cross-check.

## Applies to

- Exact rational certification of beta_3 through beta_9 for the fixed transverse local algebra.

## Limitations

- The question does not reopen beta_2, which is exactly 354 over Q.
- It does not ask for a new Kuranishi elimination.
