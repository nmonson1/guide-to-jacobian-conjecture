# Collision-pole separation for high-degree triangular words

`RMU-5E6F0002` · `theorem` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

Let S=C[P,Q,R] be the displayed Lane 5 image algebra and D≥1. For the forward two-stage word (x,y+G(x),z+H(y+G(x))), deg G>D and nonconstant H imply sigma(S) intersect B_{≤D}=C. For the reverse word, deg G,deg H>D gives pole vector (1,N,NM) and the same conclusion. For either three-letter cyclic orientation, it suffices that the final two degrees exceed D; the mixed-radix pole vector again forces only constants.

## Hypotheses

- The image algebra S and marked-root collision arc are exactly those displayed in the proof packet.
- The degree bounds for the chosen word orientation hold.

## Applies to

- The stated forward, reverse, and two oriented cyclic triangular families, uniformly in their nonzero coefficients.

## Limitations

- The result does not cover the low-weight word (x,y+x^2,z+(y+x^2)^2), all tame words, or wild automorphisms.
- The pole criterion requires a degree-D injective positive pole vector.
