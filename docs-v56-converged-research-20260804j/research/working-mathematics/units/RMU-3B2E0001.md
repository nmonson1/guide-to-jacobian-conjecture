# Exact rational second Betti number

`RMU-3B2E0001` · `computational_result` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

For the transverse local algebra of the fixed degree-seven deformation slice, exact rational row reduction of all 49 weight blocks contributing to the degree-two Koszul homology gives beta_2=354 over Q.

## Hypotheses

- The border-basis presentation, exact multiplier, weight decomposition, and 49-task queue are the hash-pinned objects in the audit receipt.
- Ranks are computed over Q; finite-field agreement is not used as the characteristic-zero proof.

## Applies to

- The second Betti number of the displayed transverse local algebra over Q.

## Limitations

- The computation does not certify beta_3 through beta_9 over Q.
- The audit replays the exact stored ranks and aggregate; it does not rerun the 49 expensive row reductions from the multiplier.
