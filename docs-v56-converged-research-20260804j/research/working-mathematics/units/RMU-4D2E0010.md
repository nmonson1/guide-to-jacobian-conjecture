# Convention-complete all-rank PRS and Hankel block theorem

`RMU-4D2E0010` · `theorem` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

Let Q be monic of degree m with nonzero constant term, let nu>=m, and let R=w^nu mod Q. With the displayed Sylvester row and column convention, the principal subresultant coefficient psc_(m-k)(w^nu,Q) equals (-1)^(k choose 2) det(H_k) and the rectangular Schur polynomial s_((nu-m+k)^k) of the roots of Q. If 0=n_0<...<n_s=m are the nonzero leading-Hankel normal indices and d_j=n_j-n_(j-1), then the complete northwest rank-profile permutation is J_(d_1) direct-sum ... direct-sum J_(d_s). Consequently the companion-PRS normal-index cells and their block-constant Smith exponents are determined in every rank.

## Hypotheses

- Q is monic of degree m, Q(0) is nonzero, and nu>=m.
- The determinant, companion and Hankel conventions are exactly those fixed in the linked proof notes.
- The full Hankel matrix H_m is nonsingular for the block-profile conclusion.

## Applies to

- Every rank cell of the complete PRS graph under the stated conventions.
- Repeated-root specializations by polynomial continuation.

## Limitations

- This theorem does not by itself prove projective gluing of all normalization charts.
- The separate T=0 and infinity-chart boundaries retain the limits stated in their own packets.
