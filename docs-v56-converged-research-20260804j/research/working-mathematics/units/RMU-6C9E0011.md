# Parameter-complete obstruction criterion for an F2 attachment block

`RMU-6C9E0011` · `lemma` · statement version `2`

## Exact statement

At a fixed normal order r, after all lower-order data are evaluated, let M_r=[C_r | P_r]:V_r^corr direct-sum V_r^fresh -> W_r be the complete finite-dimensional attachment map and let b_r belong to W_r. The intrinsic obstruction quotient is Ob_r=coker(M_r)=W_r/(im(C_r)+im(P_r)), and the affine system is solvable exactly when the forcing class [b_r] vanishes in Ob_r. Its dual space of obstruction functionals is Ob_r^dual=ker(M_r^dual), identified after choices of bases with ker(M_r^t)=ker(C_r^t) intersect ker(P_r^t). Thus a left-null functional for the zero-fresh-parameter slice descends to the intrinsic obstruction quotient only when it also annihilates every fresh-parameter column. If the coefficient field has characteristic different from 5 and contains a primitive fifth root of unity, equivariance decomposes this criterion into C5-character blocks; over a nonsplitting field the same statement holds after separable scalar extension, which preserves feasibility.

## Hypotheses

- V_r^corr, V_r^fresh, and W_r are finite-dimensional vector spaces over the coefficient field.
- All lower-order nonlinear terms have been evaluated into b_r and the known coefficients of M_r.
- C_r includes every endpoint, overlap, normalization, finite-support, presentation, and descent row required at order r.
- P_r includes every fresh order-r parameter column.
- For the character-block formulation, the data are C5-equivariant and the coefficient field has characteristic different from 5; the decomposition is taken after adjoining a primitive fifth root of unity when necessary.

## Applies to

- Any supplied finite exact attachment block, including a future order-510/520/530 F2 export.

## Limitations

- The current repository does not contain the actual high-order F2 endpoint matrices, so the lemma gives no numerical attachment verdict.
- The included order-510/520/530 contract is synthetic regression data only.
- The left kernel is the dual space of obstruction functionals, not the obstruction quotient itself.
