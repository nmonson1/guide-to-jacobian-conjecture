# For a kernel-of-an-endomorphism translation action over a Q-algebra, the invariant algebra is…

<p class="dek">For a kernel-of-an-endomorphism translation action over a Q-algebra, the invariant algebra is the kernel of the relative differential modulo im(M dual); for the bounded cubic-frame action, global invariants are the contraction of the localization at the single one-root wall q_N=0.</p>

## Exact statement

For a kernel-of-an-endomorphism translation action over a Q-algebra, the invariant algebra is the kernel of the relative differential modulo im(M dual); for the bounded cubic-frame action, global invariants are the contraction of the localization at the single one-root wall q_N=0.
