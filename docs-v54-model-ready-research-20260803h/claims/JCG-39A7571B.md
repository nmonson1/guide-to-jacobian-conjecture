## Exact statement

In the primitive fixed-component branch P=GA, Q=GB with gcd(A,B)=1 and R=(H3)_3, write t=A/B and R^4/Q^3=rho(t). If an irreducible component Gamma occurs in G with multiplicity s and in the reduced special fiber A-xi B with multiplicity m, define c_xi=ord_xi(rho) for finite xi and c_infinity=ord_infinity(rho)+3. Then 4 nu_Gamma(R)=3s+c_xi m and the coefficients satisfy sum c_xi=3.
