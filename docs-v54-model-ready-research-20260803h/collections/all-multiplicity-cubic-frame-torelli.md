# All-Multiplicity Torelli for Cubic Frames

<p class="dek">For admissible cubic frames, the map remembers the complete decorated finite scheme of deleted roots—including multiplicities—and stable equivalence adds no new identifications.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Let \((A,B)\) and \((\widetilde A,\widetilde B)\) be admissible cubic frames with \(A/c\) and \(\widetilde A/c\) nonconstant. Stable left–right equivalence, ordinary polynomial left–right equivalence, and the existence of \(u\in\mathbf C^\times\) such that \(\widetilde A(uc)=uA(c)\) and \(\widetilde B(uc)-B(c)\in cA(c)\mathbf C[c]\) are equivalent. No squarefreeness or coprimality hypothesis is required.
