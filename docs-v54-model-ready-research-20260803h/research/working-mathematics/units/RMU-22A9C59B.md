## Exact statement

The affine fiber size is three off \(S_F\), one on
\(V(\mathcal D)\setminus(T_3\cup\bigcup C_\beta)\), two on a deleted
plane away from \(V(\mathcal D)\), and zero on \(T_3\) and on every
\(C_\beta\).
