# Complete PRS graph and chartwise toroidal inverse graph

`RMU-4D2E0002` · `construction` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

For the coefficient space of monic degree-m polynomials Q with nonzero constant term, take the rational map whose factors are the projective coefficient vectors of Sres_j(w^nu,Q), 1<=j<m, and define E_(m,nu) as the normalization of its graph closure. Then E_(m,nu) is explicit, finite type, projective and separated over the coefficient space, and birational over the strongly regular locus. On the exact ordered-composition chart d, the pulled-back coarse inverse graph is the normalized blowup of one displayed monomial ideal I_d and is toroidal relative to the remaining chart parameters. PRS transfer-matrix composition gives literal cocycle equality on triple overlaps.
