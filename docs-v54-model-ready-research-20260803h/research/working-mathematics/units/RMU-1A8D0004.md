# Exact quadratic-resolvent carrier of the cubic flatness defect

`RMU-1A8D0004` · `theorem` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

Let T be the normalization in the S_3 Galois closure, Q=T^(A_3), and L the nontrivial cubic eigensheaf. Then Q is a normal finite-flat quadratic R-algebra, T=Q direct-sum L direct-sum L^[2], L^[3]=Q, and the quadratic involution satisfies sigma^*L=L^[2]=L^dual. As an R-module, the cubic trace-zero summand E is isomorphic to L. Hence Delta_F=Ext^1_R(L,R), and B is finite flat over R if and only if L is maximal Cohen-Macaulay over Q.

## Arguments

### The quadratic-resolvent eigensheaf carries exactly the cubic defect

`ARG-P1-EXACT-RESOLVENT-CARRIER` · `proof`

The A3-invariant quadratic algebra is finite flat, and the nontrivial cubic eigensheaf is the trace-zero cubic module; its MCM property is therefore equivalent to flatness of the cubic normalization.

Use the established S_3 Galois closure of the generic cubic extension. If T is its normalization and Q=T^(A_3), invariant-ring normality makes Q a normal finite-flat quadratic R-algebra. In codimension one the A_3-character decomposition is T=Q direct-sum L direct-sum L^[2], with L^[3]=Q, and reflexive extension carries this globally. A transposition interchanges L and L^[2]=L^dual. Taking transposition invariants identifies the cubic trace-zero R-module E with L. The canonical defect is consequently Ext^1_R(L,R). Because Q is finite flat over the regular ring R, Auslander--Buchsbaum compares depth over R and Q and shows that B is finite flat over R exactly when L is maximal Cohen--Macaulay over Q.
