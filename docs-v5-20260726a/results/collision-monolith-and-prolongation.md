## The central idea

The theorem-level package is centered on the following mechanism: For a dimension-minimal noninjective cubic-homogeneous Keller map, the collision algebra has a unique minimal ideal \(D\) containing the collision direction, with \(\dim D\ge\lceil(n-1)/2\rceil\). Over an algebraic closure, the Lie algebra generated on \(D\) by partial multiplications is \(\mathfrak{sl}(D)\) or \(\mathfrak{sp}(D,\omega)\).  Its supporting records isolate the ingredients that establish the statement and the qualifications that control its scope.

### Dimension-Minimal Cubic Collision Algebras Are Monolithic

A dimension-minimal cubic-homogeneous collision algebra is generated by its collision midpoint and difference, has d in A^3 and dim A/A^3 at most one, every nonzero proper ideal contains d, its minimal ideal D has nonzero internal cubic product, and dim D is at least ceil((n-1)/2).

For the minimal ideal D of a dimension-minimal cubic-homogeneous collision algebra over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, the Lie algebra generated by partial multiplications is sl(D) or sp(D,omega).
