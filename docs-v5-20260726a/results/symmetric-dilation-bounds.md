## The central idea

The theorem-level package is centered on the following mechanism: If an n-dimensional homogeneous map has a k-dimensional partial-gradient block, it admits a weak symmetric dilation in dimension at most 2n-k; in particular every polynomial map has weak symmetric-dilation dimension at most 2n-1.  Its supporting records isolate the ingredients that establish the statement and the qualifications that control its scope.
