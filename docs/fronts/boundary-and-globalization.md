# Front II: boundary reconstruction and globalization

!!! info "For researchers"
    The shortest entry route is [discriminants](../ideas/discriminants.md),
    followed by [Newton–Puiseux expansions](../ideas/newton-puiseux.md).

A noninvertible Keller map cannot fail locally at a finite point. Its failure
must be visible at infinity. The boundary is therefore not bookkeeping added
after the map is understood; it is where global noninvertibility lives.

## The story so far

In the three-sheeted example, the normalized discriminant and its marked
curves recover parameters that survive polynomial stabilization. This is a
small boundary-Torelli theorem: infinity remembers the point of the family.
A broader admissible-frame theorem recovers complete root multiplicities from
a relative-Jacobian boundary blowup.

In the plane problem, a hypothetical counterexample gives valuations and
Newton–Puiseux branches at infinity. Their leading faces can force Belyi maps,
which turn part of the boundary into finite permutation data.

The work below degree 125 illustrates the whole chain and its logical seams.
The published global classification reduces every candidate to two supports.
The project classifies the five degree-21 dessins forced by their leading-face
equation, but that face classification alone is not the global exclusion. A
separate exact reconstruction of the full support equations and terminal
certificates closes both supports in characteristic zero. Combined with the
published reduction, it proves that a plane counterexample must have maximum
coordinate degree at least 125. The project makes no priority claim over the
earlier external announcement of that bound.

## The main open question

Which finite collection of boundary invariants is complete enough to recover
an affine opening—or prove that no opening exists? Local models can be
mutually compatible and still fail a later gluing equation. Conversely, a
coordinate calculation may hide that two models represent the same intrinsic
valuation.

The distinction between **local construction** and **global attachment** is
essential. A formal solution in one boundary chart is not yet a polynomial
map, and a kernel direction is not yet gauge until it comes from an allowed
operation compatible across every overlap.
