# Front IV: framing complexity and low-degree exclusion

!!! info "For researchers"
    “Smallest” has several inequivalent meanings here. The result pages below
    keep generic degree, ordinary degree, ambient dimension, and presentation
    complexity separate.

Once counterexamples exist, “the smallest one” is not a single question. One
can minimize ordinary degree, generic degree, source dimension, number of
monomials, cubic-homogeneous dimension, or complexity of the boundary. These
measures behave differently under stabilization and normal-form reductions.

## The story so far

The first map has generic degree three. The Gallagher/Ulam constructions
realize every generic degree at least three. The project strengthens this by
showing that one can choose full symmetric monodromy in every degree, so the
examples can also be composition-prime.

Thompson's 24-variable tensor first put the counterexample in strict
cubic-homogeneous form. A rank-sensitive suspension now reaches 19 variables,
matching an independently announced endpoint of Harris Chan. The current
interval for the least cubic-homogeneous dimension is

\[
5\le N_{\min}\le19.
\]

For the characteristic-zero plane problem, the exact current lower bound is
125. The global reduction is published; an earlier external announcement and
a separate inspectable project reconstruction close the two terminal support
systems. Degree 125 itself remains possible.

The two-block stable-uniqueness theorem approaches simplicity from the other
side: within the multiplication-incidence construction, the cubic
linear-times-quadratic chart with contact order two is the only case that is
stably affine.

A different complexity phenomenon appears in deformation theory. Polynomial
equivalences may exist at every finite order while their required degrees
grow without bound. Ordinary degree is therefore not just a number attached
to a map; it can also measure the cost of moving between presentations.

## The main open questions

- What is the smallest dimension of a cubic-homogeneous counterexample?
- Which ordinary degrees are possible in dimension three?
- Is there any characteristic-zero plane counterexample at all—and, if so,
  how far above 125 must its degree lie?
- Which complexity measures are intrinsic to the finite opening, and which
  belong only to a chosen presentation?
- When can a formal change of presentation be rectified into one polynomial
  change of uniformly bounded complexity?
