# Major results

The pages below are selective. Each result changes the mathematical picture in
a way worth explaining: a new family of covers, a new normal form, a rigid
boundary invariant, or a finite obstruction in the plane problem. Proof
locations, computation, independent checks, and publication status are
collected separately in the [proof and evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.md).

## The counterexample and its descendants

- [**A counterexample in three variables**](../start/counterexample.md) — the
  explicit map, its determinant, collision, and marked-root mechanism.
- [**Keller maps of every generic degree**](every-generic-degree.md) — every
  number of generic sheets \(d\ge3\) already occurs in dimension three.
- [**A cubic-homogeneous counterexample in 24 variables**](cubic-homogeneous.md)
  — an explicit example in the classical normal form \(U+H(U)\).
- [**The separable conjecture fails in characteristic two**](characteristic-two.md)
  — first in dimension three and then in the plane.

## The surviving plane problem

- [**The announced degree bound 125**](below-125.md) — how a global reduction
  at infinity reaches two terminal support systems.
- [**Five degree-21 dessins**](degree-21-dessins.md) — a leading boundary
  equation compresses to five exact Belyi maps.

## Geometry and deformation of the first example

- [**A modulus at infinity**](stable-cubic-frames.md) — a one-parameter family
  remains distinct after arbitrary stabilization.
- [**Why the cubic two-block chart is exceptional**](two-block-uniqueness.md) —
  only the \((1,2)\) multiplication chart becomes affine space in the stated
  family.
- [**A transverse local algebra of length 584**](length-584.md) — the fixed
  degree-seven map is reduced-isolated in a bounded slice and carries a large
  nilpotent neighborhood.
