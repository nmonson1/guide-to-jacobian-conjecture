# Major results

This shelf is selective. It contains results for which a reader is likely to
want a durable explanatory page and a usable route into the proof or
certificate. It is not a claim-by-claim export of the research repository,
and the pages favor the clearest illuminating form of a theorem over its
maximum available generality.

## Results from this project

- [**Collisions detect the cubic flatness defect**](collision-flatness.md) —
  one saturation quotient in the collision nerve is exactly the dual of the
  finite normalization's Ext defect.
- [**Formal equivalence can outrun polynomial degree**](formal-effectivity.md)
  — compatible equivalences exist at every finite order although no one
  polynomial equivalence exists over the completed base.
- [**Stable classification of a family of cubic frames**](stable-cubic-frames.md)
  — a genuine boundary modulus survives arbitrary stabilization.
- [**Why the cubic two-block chart is uniquely affine**](two-block-uniqueness.md)
  — the linear-times-quadratic tangent chart is the only stably affine case
  in its two-block construction.
- [**Five degree-21 dessins**](degree-21-dessins.md) — an exact boundary
  Hurwitz problem is completely classified.
- [**A transverse local algebra of length 584**](length-584.md) — the fixed
  degree-seven map is reduced-isolated but highly nonreduced in a bounded
  deformation slice.

Each project page distinguishes the mathematical statement from its proof
source, computation package, publication status, and remaining scope. Those
are related questions, not substitutes for one another.
