# Guided topics

These pages group already published claims into mathematical developments. A topic page is navigation and exposition: it does not add a claim or upgrade any claim's review status.

This release contains only topics whose mathematical components already have public claim records. More topics will appear as their component claims are reviewed.

## Results

### [A Counterexample Over Every Field](topic-v1.2/all-fields-counterexample.md)

For every field k, the determinant-one rescaled construction formalized by Dean Cureton gives a polynomial map from k^3 to k^3 that is not injective.

*4 linked claims.*

### [Consequences for Neighboring Conjectures](topic-v1.2/consequences-for-neighboring-conjectures.md)

The Dixmier conjecture is false for the complex Weyl algebra A_n for every n at least 3.

*5 linked claims.*

### [Counterexamples of Every Generic Degree](topic-v1.2/all-generic-degrees-family.md)

For every integer d at least 3, there is a nonproper Keller map from complex affine 3-space to itself with generic degree d.

*1 linked claim.*

### [Exact Finite-Field Fiber Counts](topic-v1.2/finite-field-fibers.md)

Over every finite field F_q of odd order, every rational fiber has size 0, 1, or 3; writing N_j for the number of targets with j rational preimages, N_1+3N_3=q^3 and N_0=2N_3, with 6N_3=(q-1)(q^2+2) outside characteristic 3 and 6N_3=q^2(q-1) in characteristic 3.

*2 linked claims.*

### [Fibers, Image, and Nonproperness of the Counterexample](topic-v1.2/base-map-fibers-image-and-nonproperness.md)

The nonproperness hypersurface is the cubic discriminant, its singular/omitted locus is a cusp-type curve C1, and the fibers have the described 3/1/0 pattern.

*3 linked claims.*

### [Galois Closure, Monodromy, and Deck Transformations](topic-v1.2/base-cover-monodromy-and-deck-group.md)

The function-field extension induced by the Alpöge map has degree 3 and an S3 Galois closure, as described by the cited explicit cubic model.

*2 linked claims.*

### [Invertibility and Properness for Complex Keller Maps](topic-v1.2/invertibility-and-properness.md)

For a Keller map over the complex numbers, polynomial invertibility, properness, emptiness of the nonproperness set, and codimension at least 2 of that set are equivalent, with the empty set assigned infinite codimension.

*1 linked claim.*

### [Kraus's 1884 Conjecture and the Gap in His Proof](topic-v1.2/kraus-statement-and-proof-gap.md)

Ludwig Kraus's 1884 paper states, as its main result, the modern plane Jacobian conjecture rather than merely an earlier adjacent problem.

*2 linked claims.*

### [Small Counterexamples to the Gaussian Moments Conjecture](topic-v1.2/gaussian-moments-counterexamples.md)

The Gaussian Moments Conjecture is false in every dimension n at least 3: the cited preprint gives a five-term quartic witness in three real Gaussian variables and a six-term cubic witness in four variables.

*1 linked claim.*

### [The Alpöge Counterexample](topic-v1.2/base-counterexample-and-immediate-consequences.md)

The Jacobian conjecture is false in every dimension n at least 3.

*4 linked claims.*

### [The Characteristic-Three Degeneration](topic-v1.2/characteristic-three-degeneration.md)

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 3, the map is surjective while remaining étale and noninjective.

*2 linked claims.*

### [The Explicit Eleven-Dimensional Cubic Counterexample](topic-v1.2/eleven-dimensional-cubic-counterexample.md)

The cited map from complex affine 11-space to itself has total degree 3, 52 nonzero monomial terms, constant Jacobian determinant -2, and three displayed distinct rational inputs with one common image.

*1 linked claim.*

### [The Original Jacobian Conjecture](topic-v1.2/original-jacobian-conjecture.md)

Every polynomial map from complex affine n-space to itself whose Jacobian determinant is a nonzero constant is a polynomial automorphism.

*1 linked claim.*

### [Vitushkin's 1999 Rational Precursor](topic-v1.2/vitushkin-rational-precursor.md)

Vitushkin's 1999 rational example has an analogous multiplication-map presentation and is generically two-to-one, but its source is a punctured plane, so the resulting map has a pole.

*1 linked claim.*

### [Why the Double-Root Slice Is Affine Three-Space](topic-v1.2/double-root-affine-source.md)

In the binary-form multiplication model of the counterexample, the resultant-one double-root slice is polynomially isomorphic to affine three-space.

*3 linked claims.*
