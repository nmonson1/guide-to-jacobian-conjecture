# Mathematical stories

The guide is organized around six questions a reader might naturally ask. Each story collects externally sourced results and open problems. Precise claim records remain available deeper in the site for readers who want sources, evidence, and exact scope.

## [The Jacobian Conjecture and Its Counterexample](story-v1/conjecture-and-counterexample.md)

What the conjecture said, how the explicit counterexample refutes it, how far the refutation extends, and what remains open.

## [How the Counterexample Works](story-v1/how-the-counterexample-works.md)

The incidence construction, affine source, fibers, discriminant, nonproperness, monodromy, arithmetic behavior, and geometric precursors.

## [One Counterexample or a Whole Landscape?](story-v1/landscape-of-counterexamples.md)

Families, generic degrees, deformations, rigidity, and moduli ask whether the first example is exceptional or part of a much larger geometry.

## [What Else Changes After the Counterexample?](story-v1/neighboring-consequences.md)

The counterexample alters several conjectures connected to the Jacobian problem through reductions, equivalences, and explicit constructions.

## [Why Does the Plane Case Survive?](story-v1/plane-case.md)

The two-dimensional conjecture remains open; historical attempts and modern obstruction programs explain where the difficulty has moved.

## [How Simple Can a Counterexample Be?](story-v1/simplifying-counterexamples.md)

Ordinary degree, homogeneous reductions, classical normal forms, dimension, support, and pairing rank provide different measures of simplicity.
