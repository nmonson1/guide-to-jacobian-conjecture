# Authorship and AI assistance

The author of this guide is **Nathaniel Monson**.

AI systems have assisted throughout the project with brainstorming,
algebraic and computational exploration, source discovery, drafting,
copy-editing, code, test construction, and adversarial review. Some research
directions and proofs were developed through extended human–AI
collaboration. Where a named external contribution is involved, the page
credits that contribution independently of the tools used by this project.

AI output is not treated as a source. Mathematical claims require a proof
route, and historical claims require a citable record. Monson selects the
claims, controls the exposition and attribution policy, and is responsible
for what the guide publishes.

This single project-wide note replaces repeated AI boilerplate on individual
mathematics pages. A page adds a more specific disclosure only when the
computational or formal method is itself relevant to understanding the
result.
