# Authorship and AI assistance

The guide is edited and primarily written by **Nathaniel Monson**.
Substantial page-specific human contributions may be credited on the page or
in its source history.

AI systems have assisted throughout the project with brainstorming,
algebraic and computational exploration, source discovery, drafting,
copy-editing, code, test construction, and adversarial review. Some research
directions and proofs were developed through extended human--AI
collaboration. Where a named external contribution is involved, the page
credits that contribution independently of the tools used by this project.

AI output is not treated as a source. Mathematical claims require a public
proof route, and historical claims require a citable record. Monson controls
the guide's selection, exposition, scope, and attribution.

A result's appearance on this site does **not** mean that its proof has been
independently checked by the guide's editors. For work by this project, the
correctness claim is made in the linked paper, manuscript, formalization, or
computational release. For external work, the guide reports what the cited
public source claims and aims to represent its hypotheses, conclusion, and
status accurately.

This single project-wide note replaces repeated AI boilerplate on individual
mathematics pages. A page adds a more specific disclosure only when the
computational or formal method is itself relevant to understanding the
result.
