# A Cubic-Jet Rank Obstruction to Further Homogeneous Descent

<p class="dek">The unique scalar-monomial quadratic shift lowering the cubic jet from rank seven to six is P2=-d^2 e_a, and its quartic error is detected by an exact 13-term functional outside the cubic homological image; the natural quadratic target cleanup also fails.</p>

## Precise statement

The unique scalar-monomial quadratic shift lowering the cubic jet from rank seven to six is P2=-d^2 e_a, and its quartic error is detected by an exact 13-term functional outside the cubic homological image; the natural quadratic target cleanup also fails.
