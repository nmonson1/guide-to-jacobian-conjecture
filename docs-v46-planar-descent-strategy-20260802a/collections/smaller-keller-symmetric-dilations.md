# Can the Symmetric Descendant Be Made Smaller and Keller?

<p class="dek">The direct Euclidean 36-dimensional partial-gradient potential is not Keller when B is nonzero; obtaining a 36-dimensional Keller or cover-preserving realization is a separate self-adjoint nilpotent matrix-completion problem.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Open Problem</span> <span class="status status-draft">Draft Public</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

The direct Euclidean 36-dimensional partial-gradient potential is not Keller when B is nonzero; obtaining a 36-dimensional Keller or cover-preserving realization is a separate self-adjoint nilpotent matrix-completion problem.
