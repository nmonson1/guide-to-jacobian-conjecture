# Basepoint-Free Fixed-Factor Conic Strata Are Excluded in Degrees Five and Six

<p class="dek">Under the stated invariant-gap and basepoint-free hypotheses, the fixed-factor conic leading-form patterns (g,e)=(1,2),(3,1) in degree five and (2,2),(4,1) in degree six cannot occur for a nonautomorphic Keller map.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Claim</span> <span class="status status-draft">Proof offered — review pending</span> <span class="status">Core</span>

## Exact statement

Under the stated invariant-gap and basepoint-free hypotheses, the fixed-factor conic leading-form patterns (g,e)=(1,2),(3,1) in degree five and (2,2),(4,1) in degree six cannot occur for a nonautomorphic Keller map.
