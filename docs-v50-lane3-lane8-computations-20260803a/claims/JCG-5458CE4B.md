# The gap in Kraus's proposed proof

<p class="dek">The final step of Kraus's claimed proof is invalid because the chosen parametrization does not justify the required derivative conclusion at infinity; the unresolved issue is control of ramification at infinity.</p>

## Exact statement

The final step of Kraus's claimed proof is invalid because the chosen parametrization does not justify the required derivative conclusion at infinity; the unresolved issue is control of ramification at infinity.
