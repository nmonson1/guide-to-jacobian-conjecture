# Weighted-Lift Moduli Under Arbitrary Equivalence

<p class="dek">For every generic fiber degree n at least four, the weighted-lift counterexamples contain an (n-3)-dimensional family of pairwise inequivalent maps under arbitrary polynomial left-right equivalence.</p>

## Precise statement

For every generic fiber degree n at least four, the weighted-lift counterexamples contain an (n-3)-dimensional family of pairwise inequivalent maps under arbitrary polynomial left-right equivalence.
