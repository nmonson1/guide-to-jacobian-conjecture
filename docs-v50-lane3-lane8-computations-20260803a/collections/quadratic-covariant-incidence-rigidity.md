# Quadratic Covariant Rigidity in the Cubic Incidence Construction

<p class="dek">For every product of evaluations at two distinct points, the quadratic target hypersurface A2+kappa E_xi E_eta=c is isomorphic to A3, but its full marked-root preimage is never A3; its compactly supported Euler characteristic is never one.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

For every product of evaluations at two distinct points, the quadratic target hypersurface A2+kappa E_xi E_eta=c is isomorphic to A3, but its full marked-root preimage is never A3; its compactly supported Euler characteristic is never one.
