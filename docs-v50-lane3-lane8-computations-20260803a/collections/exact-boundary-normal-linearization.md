# Exact Normal Linearization at the Plane Boundary

<p class="dek">An exact formal change of variables makes every normal order of the single-boundary determinant equation linear; the genuine nonlinearity lies in the Newton-support constraint.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

With \(W=M_0/(AB)\), \(T=sW^{1/S}\), and \(H=(\alpha\log(B/B_0)-\beta\log(A/A_0))/S\), the full determinant equation is equivalent to \((S\,d+d\log M_0\,T\partial_T)H=\omega(1-S^{-1}T\partial_T)(W-1)\). Hence the normal orders decouple before the triangular support equations are imposed.
