# Bounded Degree Keller Scheme

<p class="dek">For fixed n,D, normalized determinant-one polynomial maps form a finite-type coefficient scheme K_{n,D}; its tangent equation at F is the coefficientwise vanishing of tr((JF)^{-1} JH), equivalently the differential of det J.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

For fixed n,D, normalized determinant-one polynomial maps form a finite-type coefficient scheme K_{n,D}; its tangent equation at F is the coefficientwise vanishing of tr((JF)^{-1} JH), equivalently the differential of det J.
