# The Quartic Target-Span-Two Ramification Filtration

<p class="dek">Under the manuscript's structural inputs, the regular, genuinely nonbinary quadratic-source, simple-, double-, fourth-, and fifth-ramification strata are closed; the only primitive binary calculation still open is the exceptional triple-ramification F4 family.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Assuming the Program 2 structural inputs, a quartic Keller map in the coprime binary-pencil locus is a polynomial automorphism whenever deg gcd(J(Q,R),J(P,R),J(P,Q)) is at most two.

### [JCG-5C216C29 · Primitive Binary Ramification Four and Five Is Automorphic](../claims/JCG-5C216C29.md)

Assume the Program 2 structural reductions place a quartic Keller map in the primitive coprime binary-pencil locus, with H4=(P,Q,0), P,Q in k[x,y]_4 coprime, R=(H3)_3 in k[x,y]_3, U=J(Q,R), V=J(P,R), W=J(P,Q), and r=deg gcd(U,V,W). If r is four or five, then the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

### [JCG-5EF7883D · The Primitive Fourth-Power Locus Adds No Quartic Counterexample Leaf](../claims/JCG-5EF7883D.md)

Assume the Program 2 target-span-two reductions and suppose a primitive coprime leading pencil contains a fourth power, normalized as H4=(x^4,Q,0) with x not dividing Q. The highest Keller equation routes the cubic and, when necessary, quadratic normal layers to an aligned pure-power, binary, or quadratic-source branch; under the recorded Program 2 inputs every resulting branch is automorphic or already excluded.

*Shared · Fourth Power Boundary · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-5E7E9377 · Zero-Minor Binary Quartic Boundaries Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-5E7E9377.md)

Assume the Program 2 binary target-span-two reduction. If one of U=J(Q,R), V=J(P,R), or W=J(P,Q) vanishes, including the separate case R=0, then the quartic Keller map is a polynomial automorphism. Consequently the ordinary common-ramification filtration may be applied only after these zero-minor boundaries are removed.

*Shared · Zero Minor Boundary · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-39A7571B · Corrected Valuation Identity for Primitive Quartic Fixed Components](../claims/JCG-39A7571B.md)

In the primitive fixed-component branch P=GA, Q=GB with gcd(A,B)=1 and R=(H3)_3, write t=A/B and R^4/Q^3=rho(t). If an irreducible component Gamma occurs in G with multiplicity s and in the reduced special fiber A-xi B with multiplicity m, define c_xi=ord_xi(rho) for finite xi and c_infinity=ord_infinity(rho)+3. Then 4 nu_Gamma(R)=3s+c_xi m and the coefficients satisfy sum c_xi=3.

*Shared · Fixed Component Input · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-48D499D2 · Genuinely Nonbinary Primitive Fixed Components Are Closed](../claims/JCG-48D499D2.md)

Assume the Program 2 four-loci reduction and the standard polynomial-centralizer theorem used in the proof note. Every genuinely nonbinary primitive fixed-component branch is closed: a fixed factor of degree two is eliminated by the corrected valuation identity or enters the existing same-fiber rank-one chart, while a fixed factor of degree one is aligned-automorphic, binary, or incompatible with the Keller condition.

*Shared · Nonbinary Fixed Components · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-DAFBEDD5 · Binary Cubic Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-DAFBEDD5.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil whose greatest common divisor has degree three. After normalization H4=(xG,yG,0) with G in k[x,y]_3, every multiplicity type of G and every cubic R=(H3)_3 yields a polynomial automorphism or a triangular degree drop; hence this stratum contains no counterexample.

*Shared · Binary Fixed Factor · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-4077AC78 · Binary Linear Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-4077AC78.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil whose greatest common divisor is a line. After normalization H4=(x^4,xB,0), with B a binary cubic not divisible by x, every quartic Keller map in the stratum is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Binary Fixed Factor · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-3C448C71 · Binary Quadratic Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-3C448C71.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil H4=(P,Q,0) whose greatest common divisor G has degree two and whose residual binary quadrics are coprime. Then the residual pencil is a secant of the Veronese conic and may be normalized to P=x^2G, Q=y^2G; the generic first-normal complement and all four exceptional divisors QF1 through QF4 contain no counterexample, so the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Binary Fixed Factor · Proof supplied*
