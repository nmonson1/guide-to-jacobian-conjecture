# Quadratic coordinate

`RMU-D49E378A` · `lemma` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

If \(f\in k[X_1,X_2,X_3]\) has degree at most two and no critical point, then
some constant directional derivative of \(f\) is nonzero.  In particular,
\(f\) is a coordinate.
