# Primitive Cubic-Frame Discriminant Content

<p class="dek">For an admissible cubic frame, the coefficient content of the discriminant in the target variables is chi=gcd(A,B^2), and the primitive nonplane discriminant Delta/chi is irreducible.</p>

## Exact statement

For an admissible cubic frame, the coefficient content of the discriminant in the target variables is chi=gcd(A,B^2), and the primitive nonplane discriminant Delta/chi is irreducible.
