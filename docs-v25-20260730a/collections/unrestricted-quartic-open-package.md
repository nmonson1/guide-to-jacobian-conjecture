### [JCG-5E7E9377 · Zero-Minor Binary Quartic Boundaries Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-5E7E9377.md)

Consequently the ordinary common-ramification filtration may be applied only after these zero-minor boundaries are removed.

### [JCG-48D499D2 · Genuinely Nonbinary Primitive Fixed Components Are Closed](../claims/JCG-48D499D2.md)

Assume the Program 2 four-loci reduction and the standard polynomial-centralizer theorem used in the proof note. Every genuinely nonbinary primitive fixed-component branch is closed: a fixed factor of degree two is eliminated by the corrected valuation identity or enters the existing same-fiber rank-one chart, while a fixed factor of degree one is aligned-automorphic, binary, or incompatible with the Keller condition.

### [JCG-DAFBEDD5 · Binary Cubic Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-DAFBEDD5.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil whose greatest common divisor has degree three. After normalization H4=(xG,yG,0) with G in k[x,y]_3, every multiplicity type of G and every cubic R=(H3)_3 yields a polynomial automorphism or a triangular degree drop; hence this stratum contains no counterexample.

*Shared · Partial Result · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-4077AC78 · Binary Linear Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-4077AC78.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil whose greatest common divisor is a line. After normalization H4=(x^4,xB,0), with B a binary cubic not divisible by x, every quartic Keller map in the stratum is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Partial Result · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-3C448C71 · Binary Quadratic Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-3C448C71.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil H4=(P,Q,0) whose greatest common divisor G has degree two and whose residual binary quadrics are coprime. Then the residual pencil is a secant of the Veronese conic and may be normalized to P=x^2G, Q=y^2G; the generic first-normal complement and all four exceptional divisors QF1 through QF4 contain no counterexample, so the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Partial Result · Proof offered — review pending*
