# For the infinite F2 complete-chain family, the first face has exactly two…

<p class="dek">For the infinite F2 complete-chain family, the first face has exactly two normalized solutions with rigid degree-three and degree-six dessins, and the exact-power lift P=S^m is impossible for every m>=2 by an odd-versus-even divisor-valuation obstruction.</p>

## Exact statement

For the infinite F2 complete-chain family, the first face has exactly two normalized solutions with rigid degree-three and degree-six dessins, and the exact-power lift P=S^m is impossible for every m>=2 by an odd-versus-even divisor-valuation obstruction.
