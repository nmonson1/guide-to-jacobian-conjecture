## Exact statement

For the discriminant surface of A(c)T^3+B(c)T^2+vT-2u with gcd(A,B)=1, the normalization conductor is exactly H^2 C[c,t], where H=3A(c)t+B(c), and its contraction is generated by B^2-3Av and -18Au-Bv.
