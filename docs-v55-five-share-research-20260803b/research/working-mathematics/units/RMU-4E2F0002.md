# Convention-complete adjacent monomial merge theorem

`RMU-4E2F0002` · `theorem` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

For adjacent monomial ideals (x^a,y^b) and (y^c,z^d), removing only the common Cartier factor makes the two normalized blowup orders agree with the simultaneous fan. Under full exceptional saturation the two orders are the circuit triangulations controlled by Delta=a0(c0-1)-d0(b0-1): Delta=0 gives the crepant flop locus, while its sign determines the flip direction.

## Hypotheses

- a,b,c,d are positive integers.
- The chosen transform convention is declared exactly as common-factor removal or full exceptional saturation.

## Applies to

- The abstract adjacent monomial-center problem.

## Limitations

- An actual all-rank PRS specialization requires its pivot valuations, merge maps, and declared transform convention.
