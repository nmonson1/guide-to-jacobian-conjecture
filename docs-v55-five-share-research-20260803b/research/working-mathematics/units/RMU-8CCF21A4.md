# For every \(r\ge2\), \[ \partial_{u_1}\Delta^{2r-3} \bigl(\mathcal Q^{2r-2}\bigr)(\bar W) = \…

`RMU-8CCF21A4` · `theorem` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

For every \(r\ge2\),
\[
\partial_{u_1}\Delta^{2r-3}
\bigl(\mathcal Q^{2r-2}\bigr)(\bar W)
=
\frac{(2r-3)!(2r-2)!}{16}\binom{2r}{r}\ne0.
\]
In particular,
\[
\partial_{u_1}\Delta(\mathcal Q^2)(\bar W)=\frac34.
\]
