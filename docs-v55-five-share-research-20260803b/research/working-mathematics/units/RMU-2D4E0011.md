# Exceptional valuation constraint at a quartic fixed-component basepoint

`RMU-2D4E0011` · `corollary` · statement version `1`

## Exact statement

In the quartic fixed-component relation P=GA, Q=GB and R^4/Q^3=rho(A/B), let E be the exceptional divisor over a common projective zero of G,A,B and write g=nu_E(G), a=nu_E(A), b=nu_E(B), r=nu_E(R). If a=b and the residue of A/B on E is not identically a zero or pole of rho, then 4r=3(g+b), so 4 divides g+b. In particular g=a=b=1 with nonconstant residual A/B is impossible.

## Hypotheses

- The map is in the primitive quartic fixed-component branch with the displayed rational identity.
- The valuation is the exceptional valuation of the blowup at the stated projective basepoint.
- The residual ratio avoids being identically a zero or pole of rho.

## Applies to

- Exceptional valuations over isolated basepoints in the quartic fixed-component branch.

## Limitations

- The corollary does not treat higher multiplicity, proportional initial forms, residual ratios at zeros or poles of rho, or prove global case-tree completeness.
- It is distinct from the basepoint-free degree-five/six conic theorem.
