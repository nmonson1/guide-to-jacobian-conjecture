# Boundary Jets and Stable Moduli in the Cubic Frame

<p class="dek">For every coprime admissible cubic-frame pair, without assuming A squarefree, stable left-right equivalence equals ordinary left-right equivalence and is classified by scaling the decorated finite scheme Z_A=Spec C[c]/(A/c) with its unit-valued jet section B|Z_A.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

For every coprime admissible cubic-frame pair, without assuming A squarefree, stable left-right equivalence equals ordinary left-right equivalence and is classified by scaling the decorated finite scheme Z_A=Spec C[c]/(A/c) with its unit-valued jet section B|Z_A.
