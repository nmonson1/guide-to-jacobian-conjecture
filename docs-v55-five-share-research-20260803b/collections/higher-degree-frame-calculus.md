# The Higher-Degree Root-Slope Frame Calculus

<p class="dek">Modulo triangular target shears, all polynomial root-slope frame potentials admit the stated unique torus-weight normal form, with necessary and sufficient layer conditions at weights m<0, m=0, m=1, and m>=2.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-partial">Partial manuscript locator</span>

## Precise statement

Modulo triangular target shears, all polynomial root-slope frame potentials admit the stated unique torus-weight normal form, with necessary and sufficient layer conditions at weights m<0, m=0, m=1, and m>=2.

### [JCG-7C0CF125 · A reduced frame potential of root degree n>=4 has leading coefficient vanishing…](../claims/JCG-7C0CF125.md)

A reduced frame potential of root degree n>=4 has leading coefficient vanishing to order at least floor(n/2)+1 at the retained infinity point; the bound is sharp and yields component degrees (3n+4,3n+3,4) for even n and (3n+2,3n+1,4) for odd n.

*Defining · Supporting Result · Proof supplied*

### [JCG-12EBB4BD · For every n>=3 the displayed explicit frame potential defines a three-variable polynomial…](../claims/JCG-12EBB4BD.md)

For every n>=3 the displayed explicit frame potential defines a three-variable polynomial Keller map of generic degree n and determinant -2, attaining the sharp frame-degree bound.

*Defining · Supporting Result · Recorded*

### [JCG-9E1E87CA · Every quartic frame has T^4 coefficient divisible by c^3 and first jet…](../claims/JCG-9E1E87CA.md)

Every quartic frame has T^4 coefficient divisible by c^3 and first jet satisfying delta+4gamma+12kappa=0; the displayed quartic realizes these conditions with determinant -2, generic degree four, and component degrees (16,15,4).

*Defining · Supporting Result · Recorded*

### [JCG-D739C229 · For every integer d at least 3, there is a nonproper Keller…](../claims/JCG-D739C229.md)
