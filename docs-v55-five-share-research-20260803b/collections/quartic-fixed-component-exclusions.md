# Quartic Fixed-Component Exclusions

<p class="dek">A corrected signed valuation closes the genuinely nonbinary primitive branch, and exact exceptional-chart sweeps close binary fixed factors of degrees one, two, and three, conditional on the upstream four-loci routing.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil H4=(P,Q,0) whose greatest common divisor G has degree two and whose residual binary quadrics are coprime. Then the residual pencil is a secant of the Veronese conic and may be normalized to P=x^2G, Q=y^2G; the generic first-normal complement and all four exceptional divisors QF1 through QF4 contain no counterexample, so the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

### [JCG-39A7571B · In the primitive fixed-component branch P=GA, Q=GB with gcd(A,B)=1 and R=(H3)_3, write…](../claims/JCG-39A7571B.md)

In the primitive fixed-component branch P=GA, Q=GB with gcd(A,B)=1 and R=(H3)_3, write t=A/B and R^4/Q^3=rho(t). If an irreducible component Gamma occurs in G with multiplicity s and in the reduced special fiber A-xi B with multiplicity m, define c_xi=ord_xi(rho) for finite xi and c_infinity=ord_infinity(rho)+3. Then 4 nu_Gamma(R)=3s+c_xi m and the coefficients satisfy sum c_xi=3.

### [JCG-48D499D2 · Assume the Program 2 four-loci reduction and the standard polynomial-centralizer theorem used…](../claims/JCG-48D499D2.md)

Assume the Program 2 four-loci reduction and the standard polynomial-centralizer theorem used in the proof note. Every genuinely nonbinary primitive fixed-component branch is closed: a fixed factor of degree two is eliminated by the corrected valuation identity or enters the existing same-fiber rank-one chart, while a fixed factor of degree one is aligned-automorphic, binary, or incompatible with the Keller condition.

After normalization H4=(xG,yG,0) with G in k[x,y]_3, every multiplicity type of G and every cubic R=(H3)_3 yields a polynomial automorphism or a triangular degree drop; hence this stratum contains no counterexample.
