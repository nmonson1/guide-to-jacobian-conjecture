\begin{abstract}
This packet records candidate repairs and exact symbolic reproductions for the
ordinary-degree-four branch of the three-dimensional Jacobian-conjecture
program.  It repairs the leading-image and four-locus arguments, supplies an
exact centralizer bridge and an $R=0$ route, treats three previously
under-documented conic orbits, gives a proper rational-cubic exclusion,
reconstructs the primitive binary $r=4,5$ high-ramification proof, and
independently closes the $r=3$, $\tau=-1$ chart.  The arguments and programs
are unrefereed.  They do not by themselves establish the global theorem
$D_{\min}\geq5$; the unconditional public interval remains
$4\leq D_{\min}\leq7$.
\end{abstract}

The packet gives a candidate global routing with exact support for several
previously inaccessible leaves.  Publication-level completion still requires
the degree-three proof-to-code crosswalk, independent reproduction of the
remaining exceptional divisors, verification of the precise plane theorem
used in the triangular exits, and specialist human review.  Accordingly no
unconditional improvement of $4\leq D_{\min}\leq7$ is asserted here.
