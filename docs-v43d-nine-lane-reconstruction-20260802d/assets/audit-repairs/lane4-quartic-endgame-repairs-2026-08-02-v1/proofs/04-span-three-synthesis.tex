\section{Completed leading-target-span-three reduction}

\begin{corollary}[Exact leading target span two]
Let $F$ be a quartic Keller map over an algebraically closed field of
characteristic zero.  If $F$ is not a polynomial automorphism, then its
leading target span is exactly two.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The repaired leading-image factorization gives
\[
H_4=G h(A,B),\qquad \deg G+e\deg A=4,
\]
and the nondegenerate leaves
\[
(e,k,\deg G)\in\{(2,1,2),(2,2,0),(3,1,1),(4,1,0)\}.
\]
A point image is excluded by the rank-one theorem.  A conic image is
excluded in all seven parabolic factor orbits: the four invariant-field
orbits from the manuscript and the three separate exact exclusions for
$G=xy,z^2,x^2$.  A proper rational cubic is excluded by the marked
cusp/node theorem, including transverse common factors and every marked
factor in the normalization pencil.  A proper rational quartic is excluded
by the two frontier theorems.  Therefore a nonautomorphic map can have only
a line as its projective leading image, which is leading target span two.
\end{proof}
