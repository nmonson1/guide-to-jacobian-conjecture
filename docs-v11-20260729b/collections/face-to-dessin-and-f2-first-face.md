# Face To Dessin And F2 First Face

<p class="dek">A primitive monomial Newton-face Jacobian equation yields an exact logarithmic derivative; under the stated coprimality and endpoint hypotheses the induced rational map is Belyi and its passport is determined by the face exponents.</p>

## Precise statement

A primitive monomial Newton-face Jacobian equation yields an exact logarithmic derivative; under the stated coprimality and endpoint hypotheses the induced rational map is Belyi and its passport is determined by the face exponents.
