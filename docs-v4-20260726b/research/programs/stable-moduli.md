# Stable Moduli

<p class="dek">The counterexample deforms; when do those deformations remain genuinely different after stabilization?</p>

<span class="status status-draft">Working research program</span>

## The mathematical idea

The counterexample belongs to explicit families.  The hard question is whether different parameters describe genuinely different polynomial maps, even after adding unused coordinates and allowing arbitrary polynomial changes on source and target.

## For a first reading

Boundary geometry supplies the invariant.  A map's nonproperness set and deleted infinity data survive equivalence more rigidly than its displayed formula, so they can separate maps that look formally similar.

## The proof strategy

The program reconstructs decorated boundary schemes from the map, computes how the parameters transform, and tests which features persist after affine stabilization.  A relative-Jacobian blowup now recovers arbitrary common-root multiplicities and gives an all-multiplicity fixed-frame Torelli theorem.

## Scope and current boundary

The stable-separation statements are presented with proofs using boundary and conductor invariants.  The finite-root classification now includes repeated roots; gluing it across strata where deleted roots escape to infinity remains open.

## Results in this program

### [A Quartic Boundary-Torelli Family](../../results/quartic-boundary-torelli.md)

Within the quartic family, stable left-right equivalence holds exactly when (rho,sigma) agrees; the normalized residual discriminant has intrinsic cusp and double-double strata and conductor divisor 2L+Q, so the family gives an affine plane of fixed-degree stable moduli.

### [All-Multiplicity Torelli for Cubic Frames](../../results/all-multiplicity-cubic-frame-torelli.md)

For admissible cubic frames, the map remembers the complete decorated finite scheme of deleted roots—including multiplicities—and stable equivalence adds no new identifications.

### [Boundary Jets and Stable Moduli in the Cubic Frame](../../results/cubic-boundary-jets-and-moduli.md)

For every coprime admissible cubic-frame pair, without assuming A squarefree, stable left-right equivalence equals ordinary left-right equivalence and is classified by scaling the decorated finite scheme Z_A=Spec C[c]/(A/c) with its unit-valued jet section B|Z_A.

### [Degree Seven Minimality Status](../../results/degree-seven-minimality-status.md)

If D_min is the minimum possible maximum ordinary total degree of a coordinate of a complex polynomial Keller counterexample in dimension three, then 4 <= D_min <= 7.

### [Equivariant Low Degree Classification](../../results/equivariant-low-degree-classification.md)

Every equivariant Keller map of degree at most six in the analyzed family is an automorphism, so seven is the sharp threshold in that family.

### [Eta Family Stable Moduli](../../results/eta-family-stable-moduli.md)

The eta-family supplies stable moduli with boundary curves of arbitrary genus.

### [Lambda Family Stable Inequivalence](../../results/lambda-family-stable-inequivalence.md)

All members of the lambda-family are pairwise stably inequivalent after separating the generic, zero, and -324 cases.

### [Moduli in the Equivariant Degree-Five Family](../../results/equivariant-degree-five-moduli.md)

At fixed generic degree five, the analyzed grading-equivariant Keller family contains a one-parameter set distinguished by critical-root configuration under grading-preserving equivalence.

### [Reciprocal Family Moduli](../../results/reciprocal-family-moduli.md)

The framed reciprocal-family moduli is naturally described by a quotient stack [V_m/G_m], with the stated tangent complex and local logarithmic complex.

### [Stable Deformation Moduli](../../results/stable-deformation-moduli.md)

For the quadratic cubic-frame slice with alpha nonzero, q=beta/alpha^2 is a complete invariant under arbitrary polynomial source-target equivalence and stabilization; the alpha=0 line is gauge-equivalent to the base map.

### [Weighted-Lift Moduli Under Arbitrary Equivalence](../../results/weighted-lift-moduli-under-arbitrary-equivalence.md)

For every generic fiber degree n at least four, the weighted-lift counterexamples contain an (n-3)-dimensional family of pairwise inequivalent maps under arbitrary polynomial left-right equivalence.

## Open problems in this program

### [Gluing Cubic-Frame Torelli at Infinity](../../results/cubic-frame-infinity-gluing.md)

The finite-root Torelli theorem is complete; the remaining compactification problem is to glue it across strata where deleted roots escape to infinity.

### [Open Problems After the Counterexample](../../results/research-agenda.md)

Natural next problems include classification of generic-degree-three examples, bounded-degree local moduli, minimum coordinate degree, nonproperness divisors, optimized cubic reduction, the plane case, and possible universality of the marked-root construction.
