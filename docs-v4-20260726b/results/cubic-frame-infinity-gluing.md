# Gluing Cubic-Frame Torelli at Infinity

<p class="dek">The finite-root Torelli theorem is complete; the remaining compactification problem is to glue it across strata where deleted roots escape to infinity.</p>

## The central idea

The problem is organized around one precise obstruction: Compactify the all-multiplicity cubic-frame classification across degenerations in which deleted roots move to \(c=\infty\), so the number of finite marked planes changes, and compare the resulting boundary with the \(\alpha=0\) stratum.  The supporting records separate known constraints from the remaining global step.

### All-Multiplicity Fixed-Frame Torelli

For admissible cubic frames with nonconstant A/c, stable left-right equivalence, ordinary left-right equivalence, and equality of the decorated Artin schemes (Spec C[c]/(A/c), B mod (A/c)) up to scaling are equivalent, without squarefreeness or coprimality assumptions.
