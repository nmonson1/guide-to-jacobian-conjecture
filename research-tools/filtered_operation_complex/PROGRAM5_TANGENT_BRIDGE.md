# Program 5 tangent bridge: the quartic functional on the full rank-six tangent plane

**Status:** exact computation from the public Program 5 formulas. This is a
restricted tangent-level result, not a 19-to-18 noncompression theorem and not
a replacement for the later 109-direction/75-automorphism packet recorded in
the Program 5 handoff.

## 1. Setup

Use the eleven variables

\[
V=(x,y,z,a,b,c,d,q,s,h,k)
\]

and weights

\[
(1,-1,-2,0,1,-2,0,-1,-2,2,-1).
\]

Let \(E\) be the vector space of weight-preserving quadratic polynomial source
fields

\[
P=\sum_{i,m} p_{i,m}\,m(V)\,\partial_{V_i}.
\]

Exact enumeration gives

\[
\dim_{\mathbf Q}E=115.
\]

Let \(Q\) and \(C\) denote the quadratic and cubic terms of the normalized
public eleven-variable map, and write

\[
[Q,P]=JQ\,P-JP\,Q.
\]

The distinguished affine solution is

\[
P_0=-d^2\partial_a.
\]

The public quartic functional is denoted \(\Lambda_4\); the quartic forcing
attached to a quadratic field \(P\) is

\[
\begin{aligned}
O_4(P)={}&JC\,P-JP\,C
 +\frac12D^2Q(P,P)\\
&-JP\bigl(JQ\,P-JP\,Q\bigr)
 -\frac12D^2P(Q,Q).
\end{aligned}
\]

All calculations below are over \(\mathbf Q\).

## 2. The two tangent spaces

### 2.1 Row-zero chart

Require the \(a,d,q,h,k\) rows of

\[
C+[Q,P]
\]

to vanish. Coefficient extraction gives 126 displayed linear equations in the
115 coefficients of \(P\). Their exact ranks are

\[
\operatorname{rank}A
 =\operatorname{rank}(A\mid b)=95.
\]

Thus the affine solution space is

\[
P_0+K_{\mathrm{row}},
\qquad
\dim K_{\mathrm{row}}=20.
\]

The previously displayed twelve-parameter family is a subspace of
\(K_{\mathrm{row}}\).

### 2.2 Tangent to the rank-at-most-six locus

At \(P_0\), the cubic-coordinate coefficient matrix of

\[
C+[Q,P_0]
\]

has rank exactly six. Linearizing the rank-at-most-six determinantal condition
inside the same 115-dimensional operation space gives a rank-93 map. Hence

\[
\dim K_{\mathrm{rank}}=115-93=22.
\]

The exact calculation verifies

\[
K_{\mathrm{row}}\subset K_{\mathrm{rank}}
\]

and therefore

\[
\dim K_{\mathrm{rank}}/K_{\mathrm{row}}=2.
\]

An adapted complement is generated by the following two quadratic fields. We
write \(e_u m\) for \(m\partial_u\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\eta_0={}&-e_b(bd)+4e_c(y^2)+\frac13e_c(qy)-e_c(cd)\\
&+2e_q(bz)+e_q(bc),
\end{aligned}
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
\eta_1={}&12e_z(y^2)+4e_b(bd)-16e_c(y^2)-\frac43e_c(qy)\\
&+4e_c(cd)+2e_k(bz)+e_k(bc)+e_k(dq).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
K_{\mathrm{rank}}
 =K_{\mathrm{row}}\oplus
  \mathbf Q\eta_0\oplus\mathbf Q\eta_1
\]

for the deterministic bases used by the exporter.

## 3. Exact quartic calculation

Let \(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_{20}\) be the exported basis of
\(K_{\mathrm{row}}\). Substitute

\[
P=P_0+\sum_{i=1}^{20}s_i\xi_i+u\eta_0+v\eta_1
\]

into \(\Lambda_4(O_4(P))\). Exact expansion in the 22 parameters gives the
identity

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_4(O_4(P))=1
}
\]

in

\[
\mathbf Q[s_1,\ldots,s_{20},u,v].
\]

In particular:

- the two linear coefficients in \(u,v\) vanish;
- the \(2\times2\) Hessian in \(u,v\) is zero;
- every mixed term involving an \(s_i\) and either \(u\) or \(v\) vanishes;
- there are no nonconstant terms of any kind.

Consequently, the two rank-six tangent directions omitted by the row-zero
normal form do **not** cancel the displayed quartic cokernel functional, even
when all quadratic couplings visible on the affine 22-plane are retained.

## 4. What this narrows

The earlier exact result established \(\Lambda_4=1\) on the
20-dimensional row-zero affine solution space. The present calculation removes
the first obvious chart-completeness concern: enlarging from that space to the
entire 22-dimensional rank-six tangent space still leaves the functional
identically equal to one.

Any remaining cancellation mechanism must therefore use something not present
in this affine tangent-plane calculation, for example:

1. higher-order bending needed to integrate the rank-six tangent directions
   inside the nonlinear determinantal locus;
2. source, target, or stable-presentation operations outside the public
   115-dimensional weight-preserving source-field model;
3. the later 109-direction/75-automorphism packet mentioned in the Program 5
   handoff but not exported in the public supplement inspected here;
4. a nonlinear change of normal form that is not represented by the chosen
   affine operation coordinates.

## 5. Boundary of the conclusion

The statement

\[
\Lambda_4=1\quad\text{on }P_0+K_{\mathrm{rank}}
\]

is an exact polynomial identity. It does **not** imply that the whole affine
plane \(P_0+K_{\mathrm{rank}}\) lies in the rank-at-most-six locus: the
vectors \(\eta_0,\eta_1\) are tangent directions, and their integration may
require higher-order corrections. Nor does the computation identify which
of the 22 directions are actual polynomial automorphisms, target operations,
or stable-presentation changes.

Accordingly, the honest consequence is a strengthened restricted obstruction:

> The public quartic functional cannot be cancelled by any direction or
> quadratic coupling on the complete first-order rank-six tangent space inside
> the published 115-dimensional source-operation model.

A full noncompression theorem still requires nonlinear integrability and the
true operation-group quotient.
