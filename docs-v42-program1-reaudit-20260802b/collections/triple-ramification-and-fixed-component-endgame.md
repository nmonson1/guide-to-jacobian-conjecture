# The Exceptional F4 and Global-Audit Endgame

<p class="dek">High ramification and fixed-component leaves now have conditional closures. The remaining calculation is the exceptional triple-ramification F4 compatibility system, followed by a global audit that every quartic map reaches a recorded terminal chart.</p>

## Precise statement

Complete the uniform extension-field compatibility calculation on the exceptional triple-ramification F4 family, including every denominator and rank-minor divisor, and independently audit that the leading-curve and four-loci reductions route every quartic target-span-two map into exactly one recorded terminal chart.

### [JCG-5C216C29 · Primitive Binary Ramification Four and Five Is Automorphic](../claims/JCG-5C216C29.md)

Assume the Program 2 structural reductions place a quartic Keller map in the primitive coprime binary-pencil locus, with H4=(P,Q,0), P,Q in k[x,y]_4 coprime, R=(H3)_3 in k[x,y]_3, U=J(Q,R), V=J(P,R), W=J(P,Q), and r=deg gcd(U,V,W). If r is four or five, then the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Resolved High Ramification Leaf · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-48D499D2 · Genuinely Nonbinary Primitive Fixed Components Are Closed](../claims/JCG-48D499D2.md)

Assume the Program 2 four-loci reduction and the standard polynomial-centralizer theorem used in the proof note. Every genuinely nonbinary primitive fixed-component branch is closed: a fixed factor of degree two is eliminated by the corrected valuation identity or enters the existing same-fiber rank-one chart, while a fixed factor of degree one is aligned-automorphic, binary, or incompatible with the Keller condition.

*Shared · Resolved Nonbinary Fixed Component Leaf · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-3C448C71 · Binary Quadratic Fixed Factors Are Automorphic](../claims/JCG-3C448C71.md)

Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil H4=(P,Q,0) whose greatest common divisor G has degree two and whose residual binary quadrics are coprime. Then the residual pencil is a secant of the Veronese conic and may be normalized to P=x^2G, Q=y^2G; the generic first-normal complement and all four exceptional divisors QF1 through QF4 contain no counterexample, so the full Keller map is a polynomial automorphism.

*Shared · Resolved Binary Fixed Component Leaf · Proof offered — review pending*
