An assessment must identify a claim, the contribution or evidence object under
review, a named assessor, a pinned reviewed version, the checking method and
scope, a conclusion, and any open objections. Links to external proofs,
computations, and formalizations are evidence records until that information is
supplied.

The Program 3 assessment is AI-assisted mathematical review, not independent
human specialist review and not a second-computer-algebra-system reproduction
of the large certificate archive.
