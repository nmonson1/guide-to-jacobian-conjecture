A collision-normalized search and a sequence of geometric exclusions isolate what a hypothetical quartic counterexample would have to look like.

## Results

### [Bounded Degree Keller Scheme](../../collections/bounded-degree-keller-scheme.md)

For fixed n,D, normalized determinant-one polynomial maps form a finite-type coefficient scheme K_{n,D}; its tangent equation at F is the coefficientwise vanishing of tr((JF)^{-1} JH), equivalently the differential of det J.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Collision Normalized Finite Search](../../collections/collision-normalized-finite-search.md)

After collision normalization, fixed-degree Keller counterexample searches become finite coefficient schemes; the message gives concrete variable counts for D=4,5,6 and recommends exact elimination or modular-to-rational computation.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Excluding Balanced Rational-Quartic Leading Images](../../collections/balanced-quartic-leading-image-problem.md)

A quartic Keller map cannot have a proper rational-quartic leading image with ramification degree two and tangent-syzygy type (2,2).

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Excluding Every Conic Leading Image in Degree Four](../../collections/quartic-conic-leading-image-exclusion.md)

No quartic Keller map in three variables has a nondegenerate conic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Excluding Proper Rational-Quartic Leading Images](../../collections/quartic-rational-quartic-frontier.md)

Assuming the established frontier reduction to the tricuspidal type (3,(1,2)) and balanced type (2,(2,2)), no quartic Keller map in three variables has a proper rational quartic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Excluding Rational-Cubic Leading Images in Degree Four](../../collections/quartic-rational-cubic-leading-image-exclusion.md)

No quartic Keller map in three variables has a nondegenerate rational cubic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Excluding the Tricuspidal Quartic Leading Image](../../collections/tricuspidal-quartic-leading-image-problem.md)

A quartic Keller map cannot have the tricuspidal quartic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Fixed-Factor Conic Exclusions in Degrees Five and Six](../../collections/degree-five-six-fixed-factor-conics.md)

The invariant-gap method excludes the basepoint-free fixed-factor conic strata of degrees five and six, and a local normal-form argument also excludes the genuine three-variable cubic-factor quintic.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Leading Curves and Normal Resonance at Infinity](../../collections/quartic-leading-curves-at-infinity.md)

For a quartic Keller deformation of a leading curve, the first nonzero normal coefficient below determinant order nine can occur only at an order divisible by the homogeneous period; in the primitive period-four case the first three normal coefficients vanish.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Rank One Quartic Theorem](../../collections/rank-one-quartic-theorem.md)

If the quartic highest homogeneous part of a degree-at-most-four three-variable Keller map has target span one, then the map is a polynomial automorphism.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [The Quartic Target-Span-Two Ramification Filtration](../../collections/quartic-target-span-two-ramification-filtration.md)

The quartic search is reduced to target span two. Regular, genuinely nonbinary quadratic-source, simple-ramification, and double-ramification strata are excluded under the manuscript's stated structural inputs; the triple branch is only partially reduced.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [The Remaining Leading-Line Geometry in Degree Four](../../collections/quartic-leading-line-reductions.md)

In the primitive coprime quartic leading-line case, the surviving normal-degree-three valuation pattern is uniquely the partition 2+1, reducing the leading data to H4=(ell^4,q2^2,0) and (H3)3=ell q2 with ell not dividing q2.

*Exact manuscript location*
