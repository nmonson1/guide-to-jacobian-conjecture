# Plane Boundary Obstructions

<p class="dek">The three-dimensional mechanism does not descend directly; what exactly blocks a plane analogue?</p>

<span class="status status-draft">Working research program</span>

## The mathematical idea

Dimension two remains open because the three-dimensional marked-root mechanism does not simply restrict to a plane.  A hypothetical plane counterexample must instead satisfy a tightly coupled boundary problem at infinity.

## For a first reading

This program studies that boundary through Puiseux expansions, log geometry, Newton faces, Belyi maps, dessins, and normal jets. Each language sees a different part of the same compatibility problem between local branches and one global polynomial map.

## The proof strategy

A primitive Newton face produces a quotient cover and a finite passport problem.  A universal linear boundary operator and its residue adjoint then propagate constraints through the normal layers.  Exact formal coordinates decouple the determinant equation before the nonlinear Newton-support restriction is restored.

## Scope and current boundary

The public degree-125 conclusion is credited to ratto3423, not claimed here.  The manuscript offers independently auditable boundary descriptions and terminal certificates.  Realizing the needed finite jets inside the filtered approximate-root subgroup and completing the global attachment remain open.

## Results in this program

### [Boundary Deformation And F2 Correction](../../results/boundary-deformation-and-f2-correction.md)

The normal-boundary determinant equation has the displayed universal order-r linear deformation operator and residue adjoint, giving an intrinsic residue interpretation of the coefficient compatibility conditions.

### [Corrected Log Geometry at Parameter Infinity](../../results/corrected-log-geometry-at-infinity.md)

The initial theorem claiming all finite quadratic-parameter incidence surfaces are of log general type was overbroad because ramification over parameter infinity overlaps the reduced boundary. The retained theorem requires squarefree behavior at infinity or the corrected log-pair hypotheses.

### [Degree Below 125 Theorem](../../results/degree-below-125-theorem.md)

Every plane Keller map with maximum coordinate degree below 125 is an automorphism.

### [Exact Normal Linearization at the Plane Boundary](../../results/exact-boundary-normal-linearization.md)

An exact formal change of variables makes every normal order of the single-boundary determinant equation linear; the genuine nonlinearity lies in the Newton-support constraint.

### [Face To Dessin And F2 First Face](../../results/face-to-dessin-and-f2-first-face.md)

A primitive monomial Newton-face Jacobian equation yields an exact logarithmic derivative; under the stated coprimality and endpoint hypotheses the induced rational map is Belyi and its passport is determined by the face exponents.

### [Puiseux Obstruction And Failed Extension](../../results/puiseux-obstruction-and-failed-extension.md)

The finite-dicritical Newton-Puiseux chart satisfies an exact coefficient recurrence; an exact two-level expansion forces a smooth embedded A1 component and hence cannot occur for a plane Keller counterexample.

### [The F2 Terminal Boundary and Its Local Flexibility](../../results/f2-terminal-boundary-and-local-flexibility.md)

For every m at least two in the F2 complete-chain family, the final regular corner has a unique normalized face polynomial q_m and a unique degree-2m Belyi boundary map with passport (m,m), (2m-1,1), (3,1^(2m-3)) and monodromy group A_{2m}.

### [Valuation And Three Dessin Exclusion](../../results/valuation-and-three-dessin-exclusion.md)

For either surviving Newton support, the (-2,1) source valuation dominates Borisov's target F_-5 with normal ramification index one and residue degree 21, excluding Borisov's Three-dessin framework for those supports.

### [Why Direct Slicing Does Not Produce a Plane Counterexample](../../results/dimension-descent-obstructions.md)

Directly slicing or graph-pulling back the three-dimensional cubic mechanism does not produce a nontrivial plane Keller counterexample: fixed nonzero slices are G_m x A1, the zero slice splits off a trivial A2 sheet, degree-two/three A2 components are excluded, and the one-pole polynomial lift cannot have constant Jacobian.

## Open problems in this program

### [A Conceptual Form of the Plane Obstruction](../../results/conceptual-plane-obstruction.md)

Recover the six decisive obstruction components canonically from the residue pole filtration or the determinant of cohomology.

### [Can the Locally Consistent F2 Boundary Model Attach Globally?](../../results/f2-global-attachment-problem.md)

Solve the two-sided band-convolution system that couples the rigid F2 final face to the affine-linear terms and determine whether any global polynomial Keller pair realizes the locally consistent terminal model.

### [Can the Nodal Puiseux Solution Be Ruled Out Globally?](../../results/puiseux-polynomial-termination.md)

Can the all-orders nodal local solution be ruled out by a finite-support or global one-double-point theorem?

### [Filtered Finite-Jet Realization at the Plane Boundary](../../results/filtered-finite-jet-realization.md)

Ordinary area-preserving jets are polynomially realizable; the remaining issue is whether the complexity-lowering jets lie in the much smaller filtered approximate-root subgroup.

### [Is the Degree-Twenty-One Divisor Unique?](../../results/three-dessin-uniqueness.md)

The identified (-2,1) divisor is the only source divisor above the generic point of F_-5, so mu=21.

### [Open Problems After the Counterexample](../../results/research-agenda.md)

Natural next problems include classification of generic-degree-three examples, bounded-degree local moduli, minimum coordinate degree, nonproperness divisors, optimized cubic reduction, the plane case, and possible universality of the marked-root construction.
