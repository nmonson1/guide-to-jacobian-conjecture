# Equivariant Low Degree Classification

<p class="dek">Every equivariant Keller map of degree at most six in the analyzed family is an automorphism, so seven is the sharp threshold in that family.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-partial">Partial manuscript locator</span>

## Precise statement

Every equivariant Keller map of degree at most six in the analyzed family is an automorphism, so seven is the sharp threshold in that family.
