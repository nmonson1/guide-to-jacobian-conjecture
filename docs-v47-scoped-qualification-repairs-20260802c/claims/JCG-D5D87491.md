# Every Finite Area-Preserving Jet Is Polynomially Realizable

<p class="dek">Over an infinite characteristic-zero field, every finite jet of a formal two-variable Jacobian-one automorphism fixing the origin is matched by a polynomial Jacobian-one automorphism composed of linear SL2 maps and polynomial shears.</p>

## Exact statement

Over an infinite characteristic-zero field, every finite jet of a formal two-variable Jacobian-one automorphism fixing the origin is matched by a polynomial Jacobian-one automorphism composed of linear SL2 maps and polynomial shears.
