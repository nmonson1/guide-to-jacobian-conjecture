# The resultant-one factor space is isomorphic to SL2 times A1_gamma, and its universal marked-root map to the simple-root binary-cubic locus is etale; ordering the roots gives an S3 torsor.

## Exact statement

The resultant-one factor space is isomorphic to SL2 times A1_gamma, and its universal marked-root map to the simple-root binary-cubic locus is etale; ordering the roots gives an S3 torsor.
