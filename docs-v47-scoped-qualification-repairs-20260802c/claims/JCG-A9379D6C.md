# The Minimal Smooth Defect End Has Two Algebraic Sign-Three Torsors

<p class="dek">For the analyzed minimal smooth defect end, the local sign-three torsor group is K_loc congruent to C[3] congruent to F_3^2, and its two nonzero directions are represented by algebraic finite covers over the henselian local end.</p>

## Exact statement

For the analyzed minimal smooth defect end, the local sign-three torsor group is K_loc congruent to C[3] congruent to F_3^2, and its two nonzero directions are represented by algebraic finite covers over the henselian local end.
