# The Categorical Quotient of Bounded Cubic Frames

<p class="dek">A differential-kernel model computes the invariant algebra, one root-escape wall controls global invariants, and a weighted graph closure supplies a separated replacement where the fppf orbit quotient is not algebraic.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status status-draft">Draft Public</span> <span class="status coverage-not_applicable">No program manuscript claimed</span>

## Precise statement

For a kernel-of-an-endomorphism translation action over a Q-algebra, the invariant algebra is the kernel of the relative differential modulo im(M dual); for the bounded cubic-frame action, global invariants are the contraction of the localization at the single one-root wall q_N=0.

## Claims in this result package

### [JCG-E48F1FF0 · All-Multiplicity Fixed-Frame Torelli](../claims/JCG-E48F1FF0.md)

For admissible cubic frames with nonconstant A/c, stable left-right equivalence, ordinary left-right equivalence, and equality of the decorated Artin schemes (Spec C[c]/(A/c), B mod (A/c)) up to scaling are equivalent, without squarefreeness or coprimality assumptions.

### [JCG-2F2C2F29 · One Polynomial Map Encodes Every Bounded Root-Translation Orbit](../claims/JCG-2F2C2F29.md)

At ambient bound N, degree-preserving cubic-frame root translations are exactly the kernel pair of (Qhat,P) mapping to (Qhat,z^N P mod Qhat); when roots escape to infinity, P decomposes uniquely into finite-root data plus the translated principal part.

### [JCG-4D953715 · The Bounded Cubic-Frame fppf Orbit Quotient Is Not Algebraic](../claims/JCG-4D953715.md)

The rank-one wall orbit sheaf B/Ann_B(pi^e) fails the strong Rim-Schlessinger pushout condition, so it is not an algebraic space; consequently the free quotient stack and the bounded cubic-frame fppf quotient along the generic one-root wall are not algebraic.

*Defining · Representability Obstruction · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-62905FD2 · Weighted Graph Closures Give Separated Spaces of Cubic-Frame Degenerations](../claims/JCG-62905FD2.md)

At a simple escape wall the graph closure is the blowup of the corresponding weighted ideal, and for an escape of length m the principal-part coordinates give a finite-type projective separated weighted degeneration space with weights (1,...,m,m+1,m,...,2).

*Defining · Separated Replacement · Proof offered — review pending*

### [JCG-2FF16912 · The Bare Stable-Orbit Moduli Problem Is Nonseparated](../claims/JCG-2FF16912.md)

For the quadratic cubic-frame family A=c+alpha c^2 and B=-2-4alpha c+beta c^2, the arcs alpha=t and beta=q t^2 have constant stable class q away from t=0 and specialize to the base class at t=0. Consequently no separated algebraic space can functorially classify the bare stable left-right orbit set while distinguishing these geometric classes.

*Shared · Coarse Moduli Obstruction · Proof offered — review pending*
