### [A Counterexample Over Every Field](../collections/all-fields-counterexample.md)

For every field k, the determinant-one rescaled construction formalized by Dean Cureton gives a polynomial map from k^3 to k^3 that is not injective.

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [A Stored Degree-21 Terminal Model Does Not Glue](../collections/stored-degree-twenty-one-terminal-no-gluing.md)

A missing layer-four operation is identified as a Newton-chart transition. In the stored specialization, the resulting layer-five-through-seven support and matching equations have no solution over the algebraic closure of the coefficient field.

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [All Generic Degrees Family](../collections/all-generic-degrees-family.md)

*result · Locator audit incomplete*

### [An Invariant Characterization of the Double-Root Orbit](../collections/double-root-orbit-characterization.md)

*open problem · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Base Cover Monodromy And Deck Group](../collections/base-cover-monodromy-and-deck-group.md)

### [Base Map Fibers Image And Nonproperness](../collections/base-map-fibers-image-and-nonproperness.md)

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Characteristic Three Degeneration](../collections/characteristic-three-degeneration.md)

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Comparison with Vitushkin's 1999 Rational Example](../collections/vitushkin-rational-comparison.md)

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Consequences for Neighboring Conjectures](../collections/consequences-for-neighboring-conjectures.md)

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Finite Field Fibers](../collections/finite-field-fibers.md)

Over every finite field F_q of odd order, every rational fiber has size 0, 1, or 3; writing N_j for the number of targets with j rational preimages, N_1+3N_3=q^3 and N_0=2N_3, with 6N_3=(q-1)(q^2+2) outside characteristic 3 and 6N_3=q^2(q-1) in characteristic 3.

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Invertibility and Properness for Complex Keller Maps](../collections/keller-invertibility-and-properness.md)

For a Keller map over the complex numbers, polynomial invertibility, properness, emptiness of the nonproperness set, and codimension at least 2 of that set are equivalent, with the empty set assigned infinite codimension.

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Kraus Statement And Proof Gap](../collections/kraus-statement-and-proof-gap.md)

Ludwig Kraus's 1884 paper states, as its main result, the modern plane Jacobian conjecture rather than merely an earlier adjacent problem.

*result · No program manuscript claimed*

### [Reconstructing the Kuranishi Ideal from Marked-Root Flows](../collections/marked-root-source-flow-reconstruction.md)

### [Regular Full-Kernel Pencils as Binary Sextics](../collections/full-kernel-regular-pencil-geometry.md)

### [Resolvent Restrictions on a Cubic Normalization Defect](../collections/cubic-resolvent-defect-exclusions.md)
