# Degree-six image algebra under explicit tame words

Lane 5 · 2026-08-03

## Scope

The current mathematics controls explicit families of tame source
automorphisms, not the whole polynomial automorphism group. The next ready
step is an exact calculation for a specified low-weight mixed-return word,
where the existing weight-separation theorems no longer apply.

These finite filtered algebras test whether low-degree information in the
image algebra survives declared source-coordinate changes. A successful
transformation law could become an intrinsic complexity budget; a single
word calculation is instead a sharp boundary example and cannot supply that
global invariant by itself.

## Setup and definitions

Let $k$ have characteristic zero, put $B=k[x,y,z]$, and define
$A=k[u_1,u_2,u_3]$ with the embedding $\iota:A\hookrightarrow B$ given by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\iota(u_1)=P&=(1+xy)^3z+y^2(1+xy)(4+3xy),\\
\iota(u_2)=Q&=y+3x(1+xy)^2z+3xy^2(4+3xy),\\
\iota(u_3)=R&=2x-3x^2y-x^3z.
\end{aligned}
\]

Write $B_{\le D}$ for polynomials of ordinary total degree at most $D$.
For a source automorphism $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_k(B)$, define the
finite-dimensional filtered piece

\[
F_D^\sigma A
=\{a\in A:\deg(\sigma(\iota(a)))\le D\}
=(\sigma\circ\iota)^{-1}(B_{\le D}).
\]

If $f_1,\ldots,f_m$ is any $k$-basis of $F_D^\sigma A$, define
$J_D^\sigma\subset A$ to be the ideal generated by all $3\times3$ minors of
the $m\times3$ matrix

\[
\left(\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial u_j}\right)_{i,j}.
\]

This ideal is independent of the chosen basis. In characteristic zero,
$J_D^\sigma=0$ exactly when the algebra generated by $F_D^\sigma A$ has
transcendence degree at most two; $J_D^\sigma=A$ if $F_D^\sigma A$ contains
a polynomial coordinate frame.

An **elementary shear** fixes two source coordinates and sends the third
$x_i$ to $x_i+f$ with $f$ independent of $x_i$. The exact packet covers:
single monomial elementary shears; arbitrary one-sided words whose nonzero
torus-weight shifts all exceed the degree-six window on the same side;
arbitrary interleaving with $z\mapsto z+ay^2$; and reduced words satisfying
the stated Taylor no-return condition. These are declared subclasses of the
tame group generated by affine maps and elementary shears. No assertion is
made for all tame words or for wild automorphisms.

## Results to use

- In the displayed frame,
  \(F_6^{\mathrm{id}}A=\operatorname{span}_k\{1,u_2,u_3\}\),
  equivalently $\iota(A)\cap B_{\le6}=\operatorname{span}_k\{1,Q,R\}$.
- Every single monomial elementary shear is classified exactly. The bounded
  calculation uses the common-fibre difference matrix on the $84$ source
  monomials of degree at most six. It has $81$ cases: $10$ have rank $82$ and retain
  $\operatorname{span}\{1,\sigma(R)\}$; $71$ have rank $83$ and retain only
  constants. The resonant family $z\mapsto z+cy^2$ is also exact for every
  coefficient $c$.
- Give $x,y,z$ weights $-1,1,2$. Then $P,Q,R$ have weights $2,1,-1$ and
  $B_{\le6}$ occupies the interval $[-6,12]$. The one-sided theorem covers
  arbitrary finite words whose nonzero shifts are all at least $19$ or all
  at most $-19$. The Taylor no-return theorem also covers mixed signs when
  every nonzero ordered Taylor shift stays more than $18$ away from the
  original window.
- None of these theorems covers a low-weight word whose ordered Taylor terms
  return to the interval $[-6,12]$, all tame automorphisms, or wild
automorphisms.

## Baseline counterexample

The displayed map \(F_0=(P,Q,R)\) satisfies

\[
\det J(P,Q,R)=-2
\]

and has the exact rational collision

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_0(0,0,-1/4)
&=F_0(1,-3/2,13/2)\\
&=F_0(-1,3/2,13/2)=(-1/4,0,0).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus it is a noninjective Keller map over \(\mathbf Q\), hence over
\(\mathbf C\). The [baseline exact verifier](lane-5-source-packet.md#source-9ec82063f46b1a95)
checks the symbolic determinant and every displayed image equality. This
baseline is established input; the tasks below concern how its image algebra
meets degree-six source filtrations.

## Example: the resonant elementary shear

For $\rho_c(x,y,z)=(x,y,z+cy^2)$ and $c\ne0$, the exact weight-space
certificate gives

\[
F_6^{\rho_c}A=\operatorname{span}_k\{1,u_3\},
\qquad J_6^{\rho_c}=0.
\]

At $c=0$, the filtered piece is
$\operatorname{span}_k\{1,u_2,u_3\}$. This is a complete one-parameter
example, not a statement about compositions with an opposite-weight factor.

## Live problem

Let

\[
\Psi(x,y,z)=\bigl(x,\ y+x^2,\ z+(y+x^2)^2\bigr).
\]

This is the explicit two-factor tame word
$\exp(x^2\partial_y)\exp(y^2\partial_z)$ in the convention used by the
reduced-word packet. Its two weight shifts are $-3$ and $0$, so the existing
one-sided and no-return theorems do not decide it. Compute $F_6^\Psi A$ and
$J_6^\Psi$ exactly.

Inputs: L5-T1 and the explicit family

\Psi_{N,M}(x,y,z)
=\bigl(x,\ y+x^N,\ z+(y+x^N)^M\bigr),
\qquad 2\le N,M\le6,
\]

together with the same exact fibre-difference method and the
[coefficient-transport theorem](lane-5-source-packet.md#source-e9c67bd9a2c5c268).

Deliverable: for all $25$ pairs $(N,M)$, exact dimensions and bases for
$F_6^{\Psi_{N,M}}A$, the corresponding $J_6^{\Psi_{N,M}}$ classification,
and a proof or certificate for every row of the table.

Dependencies: L5-T1's composition-aware implementation and immutable
source-coordinate ordering.

Limits: the finite table is a discovery and boundary map; extrapolation to
larger exponents requires a separate theorem.

Alternative connections: a module intrinsic to the finite cover in Lane 1
is relevant only if its pullback under these declared source automorphisms is
constructed explicitly.

## Limits

The available theorems concern the displayed image algebra and precisely
declared tame subclasses. They do not provide a presentation-independent
minimum degree, classify every tame word, or say anything about the wild part
of $\operatorname{Aut}_k(k[x,y,z])$.
