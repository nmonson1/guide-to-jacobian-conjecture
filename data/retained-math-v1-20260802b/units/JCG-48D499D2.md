Every genuinely nonbinary primitive fixed-component branch is closed: a fixed factor of degree two is eliminated by the corrected valuation identity or enters the existing same-fiber rank-one chart, while a fixed factor of degree one is aligned-automorphic, binary, or incompatible with the Keller condition.
