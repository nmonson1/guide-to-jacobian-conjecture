Assume the Program 2 reductions reach a binary leading pencil whose greatest common divisor has degree three. After normalization H4=(xG,yG,0) with G in k[x,y]_3, every multiplicity type of G and every cubic R=(H3)_3 yields a polynomial automorphism or a triangular degree drop; hence this stratum contains no counterexample.
