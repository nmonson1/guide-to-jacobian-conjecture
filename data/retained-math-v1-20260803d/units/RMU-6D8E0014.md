# No plane Keller counterexample below maximum coordinate degree 125

`RMU-6D8E0014` · `corollary`

## Mathematical record

`RMU-6D8E0014` · `corollary`

There is no characteristic-zero plane Keller counterexample (P,Q) with max(deg P,deg Q)<125.

Hypotheses:

- The base field has characteristic zero and [P,Q] is a nonzero constant.
- The published GGHV Theorem 2.1, Proposition 4.1, Proposition 4.3, and Corollary 5.7 are used with their stated hypotheses.
- The internal five-face classification, direct two-support closure, and compact toric terminal theorem are used exactly as cited.

Dependencies:

- `depends_on` [`RMU-6D8E0013`](../units/RMU-6D8E0013.md): The last two normalized supports left by the literature reduction are empty.

Support:

- **proof:** GGHV leave the degree pair (72,108), exclude the (9,27) complete-chain case, and route the remaining (8,28) case to two normalized supports; the direct support theorem excludes both. — [`manuscripts/06-plane-boundary/appendices/degree-twenty-one-certificates.tex#cor:plane-degree-below-125`](../../proof-sources/06-plane-boundary/appendices/degree-twenty-one-certificates.md#label-cor-plane-degree-below-125)
  - Does not establish: Any exclusion at maximum coordinate degree 125 or above.
- **citation:** Primary source for the below-125 degree inventory, the (8,28) two-support reduction, and the (9,27) exclusion. — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14178>
  - Does not establish: The two internal support exclusions proved by RMU-6D8E0013.

Limitations:

- No novelty or priority claim is made for the degree-125 bound.
- The literature reduction is source-checked and cited, not independently reproved in this repository.
- The conclusion is a finite degree bound, not the plane Jacobian conjecture.
