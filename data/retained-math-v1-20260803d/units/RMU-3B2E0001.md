## Mathematical record

`RMU-3B2E0001` · `computational_result`

For the transverse local algebra of the fixed degree-seven deformation slice, exact rational row reduction of all 49 weight blocks contributing to the degree-two Koszul homology gives beta_2=354 over Q.

Hypotheses:

- The border-basis presentation, exact multiplier, weight decomposition, and 49-task queue are the hash-pinned objects in the audit receipt.
- Ranks are computed over Q; finite-field agreement is not used as the characteristic-zero proof.

Support:

- **certificate:** Write-once receipt for the exact rational 49-block aggregate, including queue, source, output, and executable hashes. — `research-notes/program3-rational-beta2-audit-20260803-v1/README.md`
  - Does not establish: Any exact rational value of beta_3 through beta_9.
- **proof:** The manuscript appendix states the weightwise rank formula and exact rational conclusion. — [`manuscripts/03-local-rigidity/appendices/border-basis-and-betti.tex#thm:rational-beta-two`](../../proof-sources/03-local-rigidity/appendices/border-basis-and-betti.md#label-thm-rational-beta-two)
  - Does not establish: The remaining rational Betti numbers.

Limitations:

- The computation does not certify beta_3 through beta_9 over Q.
- The audit replays the exact stored ranks and aggregate; it does not rerun the 49 expensive row reductions from the multiplier.
