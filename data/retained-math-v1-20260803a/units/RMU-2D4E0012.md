## Mathematical record

`RMU-2D4E0012` · `lemma`

Let $H_4\ne0$ and suppose the closure $C_4(F)$ of the projective leading image is a curve of degree $e\ge1$. Then there are coprime forms $A,B$ of a common degree $k\ge1$, degree-$e$ binary forms $h=(h_0,h_1,h_2)$ without a common root giving the normalization map of $C_4(F)$, and a form $G$ such that $H_4=G h(A,B)$ and $\deg G+ek=4$. For a nondegenerate image the numerical leaves are $(e,k,\deg G)=(2,1,2),(2,2,0),(3,1,1),(4,1,0)$; $e=1$ is the leading-target-span-two locus.

Hypotheses:

- The ground field has characteristic zero.
- The nonzero homogeneous triple $H_4$ has projective image closure equal to an integral curve $C_4(F)$.

Support:

- **proof:** Choose the normalization of the actual image curve, pull its rational inverse back to the source, prove the substituted coordinate triple has no common divisor, and compare projective triples. — [`manuscripts/02-low-degree/main.tex#lem:leading-image`](../../proof-sources/02-low-degree/main.md#label-lem-leading-image)
  - Does not establish: Exhaustive routing through all later quartic case-tree charts.
- **proof:** Euler's identity and the projective differential show that a singular homogeneous Jacobian has projective image dimension at most one; target span three excludes a point or line. — [`manuscripts/02-low-degree/main.tex#cor:span-three-curve-entry`](../../proof-sources/02-low-degree/main.md#label-cor-span-three-curve-entry)
  - Does not establish: Any lower-degree Keller compatibility equation.

Limitations:

- The lemma classifies the numerical leading-image factorization only; it does not prove that every numerical leaf is compatible with the lower Keller layers.
- Relative algebraic closure alone does not make an arbitrary parametrization of the image birational.
