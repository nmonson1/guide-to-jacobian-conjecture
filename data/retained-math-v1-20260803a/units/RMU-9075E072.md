# Complete stable classification of the quadratic cubic-frame family

`RMU-9075E072` · `theorem`

## Mathematical record

`RMU-9075E072` · `theorem`

For the quadratic cubic-frame family G_(alpha,beta), the alpha=0 line is one ordinary left-right orbit and is stably distinct from every alpha!=0 member. For alpha,alpha'!=0, the maps G_(alpha,beta) and G_(alpha',beta') are stably polynomially left-right equivalent if and only if beta/alpha^2=beta'/alpha'^2. In particular, for the normalized family G_q, one has G_q stably equivalent to G_q' exactly when q=q'.

Hypotheses:

- The ground field is C and stable equivalence means polynomial left-right equivalence after adjoining finitely many identity variables.
- G_(alpha,beta) is the explicit quadratic cubic-frame family defined in manuscripts/04-stable-moduli/main.tex.

Support:

- **proof:** Intrinsic nonproperness divisor, normalization, marked-cylinder rigidity, exceptional-incidence separation and the explicit alpha=0 gauge. — [`manuscripts/04-stable-moduli/main.tex#thm:main`](../../proof-sources/04-stable-moduli/main.md#label-thm-main)
  - Full source and surrounding context: [`manuscripts/04-stable-moduli/main.tex#thm:main`](../../proof-sources/04-stable-moduli/main.md#label-thm-main)
  - Does not establish: A classification outside the displayed quadratic cubic-frame family.
- **program:** Exact symbolic verifier for the discriminant, normalization, recovery, gradient, marked-line, exceptional and gauge identities. — [`manuscripts/04-stable-moduli/code/main-modulus/verify_q_full_orbit.py`](../../proof-sources/04-stable-moduli/code/main-modulus/verify_q_full_orbit.md)
  - Full source and surrounding context: [`manuscripts/04-stable-moduli/main.tex#thm:main`](../../proof-sources/04-stable-moduli/main.md#label-thm-main)
  - Does not establish: The conventional normalization and marked-cylinder arguments by computation alone.

Limitations:

- The theorem does not classify every generic-degree-three Keller map or every cubic-frame family.
