## Setup and research posture

This program studies how the highest homogeneous part of a normalized
three-variable Keller map constrains the entire map. The useful mental model
is a deformation at infinity: the leading map is singular, while every lower
determinant coefficient must cancel with exceptional precision. Geometry
organizes the possible leading images; exact coefficient calculations kill
or expose the terminal charts.

The order of reductions matters. Determine the leading target span and image
geometry before entering binary-pencil or fixed-component coordinates. A
normal form is useful only after its membership in the intended stratum has
been checked directly. A finite elimination establishes exactly the chart it
parametrizes and nothing on divisors that were inverted during normalization.

This is a two-sided search. A positive-dimensional family that survives all
honest Keller layers should be promoted from “failed exclusion” to a
construction lead. A mechanism-independent counterexample would be at least
as valuable as another excluded stratum.

## Strategy and payoff

The main structural task is a single global routing argument. It should begin
with the possible leading target spans, pass through the leading-curve and
composition reductions, and end at every ramification or fixed-component
chart. Each edge must carry its hypotheses; overlaps need explicit ownership;
each leaf needs a mathematical or computational locator. This work is more
valuable now than another isolated symbolic calculation because many terminal
calculations already exist while their global placement remains the fragile
part.

Independent reproduction is the second priority. Existing exact programs are
useful evidence, but several descend from one reconstructed symbolic lineage.
A second derivation should rebuild the branch equations from the conventional
geometric input and compare chart ownership as well as final expressions.

The third priority is proof-to-code correspondence. For each computer-assisted
leaf, identify the precise proof chart, program group, coefficient domain,
inverted factors, and limitation. A passing script must never silently stand
in for the geometric argument that its chart is exhaustive.

## Connections

The program connects to filtered local rigidity through determinant-arc and
deformation calculations, to stable moduli through boundary strata and
normalization choices, and to the plane-boundary program through valuation
and ramification bookkeeping. Presentation-sensitive companion or Jordan
budgets can be valuable consistency checks, but they should not be mistaken
for invariants under arbitrary polynomial left-right equivalence.

Degree-five and degree-six calculations are useful as reconnaissance and as
tests of reusable structural lemmas. They do not justify skipping unresolved
degree-four routing. Likewise, finite-field searches can locate components or
counterexamples to a proposed normal form, but they do not prove
characteristic-zero nonexistence.

## Current priorities and research freedom

The current attention order is:

1. write and audit the global case tree;
2. independently reproduce the encoded degree-three endgame;
3. audit proof-to-program correspondence for the strongest ramification and

These are attention coordinates, not mandatory task capsules. Pursue a
stronger theorem, cleaner invariant, cross-program connection, counterexample,
or unlisted construction when it has higher leverage. Explain which retained
