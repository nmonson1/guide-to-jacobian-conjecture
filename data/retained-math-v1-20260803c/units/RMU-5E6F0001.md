## Mathematical record

`RMU-5E6F0001` · `theorem`

At the generic point rho:A→L of a branch component, the intrinsic tangent object is Der_{k,rho}(A,L). Frame changes and basis changes act on its derivative matrix by right and left multiplication, so the maximal-minor rank-drop ideal is intrinsic. Under a declared equivalence, filtered spaces transport functorially in the available direction; equality requires preservation of the degree cutoff in both directions.

Hypotheses:

- The marked branch data and their compatible map are specified.
- Any claimed equality of filtered pieces includes preservation of the ordinary-degree filtration in both directions.

Support:

- **proof:** Conormal-duality and change-of-basis proof of the transformation law. — `research-notes/lane5-collision-transport-20260803-v1/RELATIVE_DERIVATION_COVARIANCE.md`
  - Does not establish: A presentation-independent minimum degree for all source automorphisms.

Limitations:

- Ordinary total degree is not thereby made invariant under the full polynomial automorphism group.
- A single frame determinant is only defined up to the declared trivializations.
