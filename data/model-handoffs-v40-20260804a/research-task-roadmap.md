# Current research-task roadmap

This compact view is compiled from the same retained task objects as
the nine lane pages. **Ready now** means that every required input is
public. **Blocked** names the missing mathematical data and, when
applicable, the task that should supply them. Effort labels describe
scale, not confidence. Follow a lane link for setup, known results,
examples, and mathematical context.

## Lane 1: Cubic normalization defects

[Open the full lane](../handoffs/cubic-flatness-normalization-defects.md)

### Find the Keller-specific cubic-resolvent constraint

`TSK-L1-KELLER-RESOLVENT-CARRIER` · **Ready now** · proof, exploration · open ended

Prove a Keller-specific MCM criterion excluding the known elliptic-cone model, or construct an actual cubic Keller normalization with a non-MCM carrier and its source-opening Čech defect.

### Test the fixed type-IV Keller realizability gate

`TSK-L1-TYPEIV-REALIZABILITY` · **Ready now** · proof, exploration · sustained

Construct a compatible affine Keller opening of the explicit type-IV cone or prove that the Euler lift and boundary geometry exclude every such opening.

`TSK-L2-PRS-ADJACENT-SPECIALIZATION-V3` · **Blocked** · proof, computation · sustained

### Test a bounded boundary-Torelli language on the PRS family

`TSK-L2-BOUNDED-TORELLI-V2` · **Blocked** · proof, exploration · open ended

Fix a finite language of regular or rational boundary functions with declared pole or multidegree bounds and decide whether it separates generic members of the supplied PRS family under its fixed marked-input action.

Depends on: `TSK-L2-PRS-RELEVANT-MARKED-FAMILY-V2`

Blocked on:

- No positive-dimensional, structurally PRS-relevant family with generically non-isomorphic normalized marked boundaries has yet been supplied.
