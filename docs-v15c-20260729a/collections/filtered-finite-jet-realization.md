# Filtered Finite-Jet Realization at the Plane Boundary

<p class="dek">Ordinary area-preserving jets are polynomially realizable; the remaining issue is whether the complexity-lowering jets lie in the much smaller filtered approximate-root subgroup.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Open Problem</span> <span class="status status-draft">Draft Public</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Determine whether the finite jets solving the triangular support equations are realized by the valuation-filtered subgroup generated by admissible complete-chain approximate-root operations, compatibly across all relevant boundary charts.
