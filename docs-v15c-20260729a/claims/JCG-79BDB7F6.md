# A Cubic Defect Forces a Nonisolated Quadratic-Resolvent Singularity

<p class="dek">For the quadratic resolvent Q of a complex cubic Keller normalization, an isolated singularity cannot support a nonflat cubic defect; moreover, three-torsion in Cl(Q) is detected at the height-two singular primes.</p>

## Exact statement

For the quadratic resolvent Q of a complex cubic Keller normalization, an isolated singularity cannot support a nonflat cubic defect; moreover, three-torsion in Cl(Q) is detected at the height-two singular primes.
