## Exact statement

For the degree-eight local Kuranishi problem, the residual 28-dimensional slice reduces to weights -2 and -1; weight -2 is eliminated over Q, while weight -1 survives to fourth order and has only modular death evidence at order six.

Statement version `1`. The public tag is stable; statement revisions increment the version rather than replacing the tag.

## Evidence boundary

The weight -2 elimination is an exact rational calculation. The weight -1 sector is known exactly to survive through order four. Later modular rank calculations are evidence only: they do not prove characteristic-zero death, exclusion of every first-normal branch, or saturation by the known affine and shear components.

The [Program 3 v13 corrigendum](../research/program-3-v13-corrigendum.md) withdraws the stronger conclusion printed in Appendix C.3 and records the replacement proof boundary.
