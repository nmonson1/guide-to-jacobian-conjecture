# Cubic Keller Maps Have Only the U1, U2, and B Divisorial Types

<p class="dek">For a complex generic-degree-three Keller map, every target prime divisor has a retained unramified sheet and hence only the U1, U2, or B deleted-sheet type; a deleted three-cycle is impossible, and the generic monodromy is S3.</p>

## Exact statement

For a complex generic-degree-three Keller map, every target prime divisor has a retained unramified sheet and hence only the U1, U2, or B deleted-sheet type; a deleted three-cycle is impossible, and the generic monodromy is S3.
