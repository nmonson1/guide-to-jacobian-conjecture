## Mathematical record

`RMU-0DFDE6EE` · `theorem`

Assume that the completed quadratic resolvent along its singular curve has a
one-parameter hypersurface model
\[
R=\C[[t,x_0,x_1,x_2]]/(\phi_t)
\]
whose central projective cubic is smooth, and that the transverse
three-torsion class is represented by a section of the relative
three-torsion group scheme of that smooth cubic family.  Then the associated
rank-one reflexive three-torsion module is MCM.  Hence this local model
cannot support a cubic normalization defect.

Support:

- **proof:** A proof body follows this labelled manuscript statement. — Inline support is reproduced on the unit record.

Formal coordinate changes remove the higher transverse terms while
preserving the cubic-axis model.  The torsion section extends uniquely in
the smooth relative three-torsion group scheme.  Its Moore/section module is
a rank-one MCM module with the prescribed transverse class.  The
codimension-two uniqueness in
\cref{thm:isolated-resolvent-exclusion} identifies it with \(L\), and
\cref{prop:resolvent-mcm-criterion} gives flatness.
