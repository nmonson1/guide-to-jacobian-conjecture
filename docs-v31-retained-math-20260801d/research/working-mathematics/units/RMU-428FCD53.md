## Mathematical record

`RMU-428FCD53` · `theorem`

Let \(R\) be a DVR with uniformizer \(\pi\), let \(e\ge 1\), and let
\[
K_e=\operatorname{Spec} R[s]/(\pi^es)
\]
act on \(\mathbb A^1_R\) by translation.  Its fppf orbit sheaf is not an algebraic
space, and the quotient stack is not an algebraic stack.  Consequently the
bounded cubic-frame fppf quotient has the same failure along its generic
one-root wall.

Support:

- **proof:** A proof body follows this labelled manuscript statement. — Inline support is reproduced on the unit record.

The orbit sheaf on an \(R\)-algebra \(B\) is
\[
B/\operatorname{Ann}_B(\pi^e)\simeq\pi^eB.
\]
For the standard pushout
\[
B=R/(\pi^{e+1})\times_{R/(\pi^e)}R/(\pi^{e+1}),
\]
its value maps diagonally into \(k\oplus k\), rather than surjectively.
This violates the strong Rim--Schlessinger condition for algebraic stacks.
Algebraic stacks satisfy that condition by
\cite[Lemma 98.18.2, Tag 0CXN]{stacks0CXN}.
The action is free, so an algebraic quotient stack would be an algebraic
space.  At the generic cubic-frame wall, Smith normal form gives this
rank-one action times an unaffected affine factor.
