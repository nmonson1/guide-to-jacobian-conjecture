`RMU-DD6B3EDC` · `proposition`

Let \(F\colon\A^3\to\A^3\) be a generic-degree-three Keller map, and let
\(\overline X\to\A^3\) be the normalization of the target in its function
field.  If \(\overline X\to\A^3\) is finite flat of degree three, then over
the simple-root locus it is a pullback of the universal
resultant-one marked-root cover.  Recovering the original affine source from
\(\overline X\) additionally requires a theorem identifying the entire
deleted boundary.

- **proof:** A proof body follows this labelled manuscript statement. — Inline support is reproduced on the unit record.

After base change to the source function field, the chosen sheet splits off
from the cubic algebra and leaves a quadratic algebra.  Finite flatness
makes the trace-zero part locally free of rank two, so the multiplication
data are encoded by a binary cubic, equivalently by the classical
triple-cover data.  Resultant normalization then gives the map to the
universal factor space.  This identifies the finite cover on the
simple-root locus.  It does not prove that the given affine source deletes
exactly the ramification and expected infinity components; unramified sheets
may also be absent.  That boundary-completeness statement is a separate
hypothesis.
