## Mathematical record

`RMU-9D054DC3` · `theorem`

If \(Q\) has an isolated singularity at a putative defect point, then the
cubic normalization is flat there.  More generally, restriction to the
height-two singular primes is injective on \(\Cl(Q)[3]\).

- **proof:** A proof body follows this labelled manuscript statement. — Inline support is reproduced on the unit record.

For a three-dimensional local hypersurface with an isolated singularity,
Dao's torsion-freeness theorem for the Picard group of the punctured spectrum
shows that \(L\) is trivial there.  Reflexivity extends the trivialization
across the closed point, and \cref{prop:resolvent-mcm-criterion} applies.

For the second assertion, let \(M\) be a rank-one reflexive
three-torsion module whose class vanishes at every height-two singular
prime.  It is free at every other nonmaximal prime: height-one localizations
are DVRs and height-two regular localizations are factorial.  Thus \(M\) is
a three-torsion line bundle on the punctured spectrum.  Dao's theorem makes
it trivial, and reflexivity again extends the trivialization.
