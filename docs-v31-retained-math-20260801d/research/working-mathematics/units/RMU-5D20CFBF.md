`RMU-5D20CFBF` · `proposition`

If \(L\) is maximal Cohen--Macaulay over \(Q\), then \(B\) is flat over
\(A\).

Support:

- **proof:** A proof body follows this labelled manuscript statement. — Inline support is reproduced on the unit record.

The hypersurface \(Q\) is Gorenstein, so \(L^\vee\simeq L^{[2]}\) is MCM
whenever \(L\) is.  Hence \(T\) has depth three over \(A\) and is
\(A\)-free by Auslander--Buchsbaum.  The cubic algebra \(B=T^{C_2}\) is an
\(A\)-direct summand of \(T\), and is therefore free.
