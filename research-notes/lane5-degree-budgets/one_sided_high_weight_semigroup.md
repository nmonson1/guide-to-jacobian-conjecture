# One-sided high-weight source-semigroup theorem

Let

```text
S = k[P,Q,R] subset B = k[x,y,z]
```

be the displayed Keller image algebra over a characteristic-zero field. Give
`B` the source-torus grading

```text
wt(x)=-1,  wt(y)=1,  wt(z)=2.
```

Then `S` is graded, with

```text
wt(P)=2,  wt(Q)=1,  wt(R)=-1,
```

and the ordinary degree-six space

```text
V = B_{<=6}
```

is supported in the weight interval `[-6,12]`, of width `18`.

The theorem below extends the same-direction polynomial-shear result to
arbitrary finite words in noncommuting homogeneous source shears, provided all
weight shifts point to the same side of the degree-six window.

## Abstract window-separation lemma

Let `B=direct_sum_w B_w` be a `Z`-graded algebra, let `A subset B` be a graded
subalgebra, and let

```text
V subset direct_sum_{a<=w<=b} B_w
```

be a finite-dimensional graded vector space. Put `W=b-a`.

Let

```text
sigma = exp(D_m) ... exp(D_1),
```

where every `D_i` is a homogeneous locally nilpotent derivation of `B`, with
weight `e_i`. Assume either

```text
e_i > W for every i,
```

or

```text
e_i < -W for every i.
```

Then

```text
sigma(A) intersect V  subset  A intersect V.
```

No commutativity among the derivations is assumed.

### Proof

Take `g in sigma(A) intersect V` and write its weight decomposition as

```text
g = sum_{a<=w<=b} g_w.
```

Since `h=sigma^(-1)(g)` belongs to `A`, expand

```text
h = exp(-D_1) ... exp(-D_m)(g).
```

The expansion is finite on `g`, because the `D_i` are locally nilpotent. Every
term has the form of an ordered derivative word applied to some `g_w`. Its
weight is

```text
w + n_1*e_1 + ... + n_m*e_m,
```

with all `n_i>=0`. The zero-order term has weight `w`.

Suppose first that every `e_i>W`. Any nonzero derivative word shifts weight by
at least `W+1`; hence its weight is greater than

```text
a+W = b.
```

Therefore the component of `h` in weight `w in [a,b]` is exactly `g_w`.
Because `h` lies in the graded algebra `A`, each `g_w` lies in `A`. Thus
`g lies in A intersect V`.

If every `e_i<-W`, every nonzero derivative word has weight less than

```text
b-W = a,
```

and the same argument applies. This proves the inclusion.  ∎

## Lane 5 corollary

Let `U_+` be the semigroup generated by exponentials of homogeneous locally
nilpotent source derivations of weight at least `19`, and let `U_-` be the
analogous semigroup for weights at most `-19`. For every

```text
sigma in U_+ union U_-,
```

one has

```text
sigma(S) intersect B_{<=6}
  subset span_k{1,Q,R}.
```

Consequently

```text
trdeg_k k[sigma(S) intersect B_{<=6}] <= 2.
```

In particular, no element of either one-sided semigroup can expose three
algebraically independent target functions of source degree at most six.
This conclusion permits arbitrary coefficients, arbitrary word length,
arbitrary ordering, and noncommuting coordinate directions.

### Elementary generators covered

For an elementary derivation

```text
D = x_j^N partial_{x_i},
```

its weight is

```text
wt(D)=N*wt(x_j)-wt(x_i).
```

Thus the positive semigroup may use arbitrary words in

```text
x -> x+c*y^N,   N>=18;
z -> z+c*y^N,   N>=21;
y -> y+c*z^N,   N>=10;
x -> x+c*z^N,   N>=9,
```

while the negative semigroup may use arbitrary words in

```text
z -> z+c*x^N,   N>=17;
y -> y+c*x^N,   N>=18.
```

The directions inside either list need not commute. Polynomial shears with
several high-weight monomials are included by factoring their commuting
same-direction summands.

## Interleaving the resonant shear

The weight-zero family

```text
rho_a: z -> z+a*y^2
```

commutes with the source torus, so `rho_a(S)` is graded. Its exact degree-six
intersection is already certified:

```text
rho_0(S) intersect B_{<=6} = span{1,Q,R},

rho_a(S) intersect B_{<=6} = span{1,rho_a(R)}  for a!=0.
```

Consider an arbitrary finite word whose nonzero-weight factors all have
weight at least `19`, or all have weight at most `-19`, and whose remaining
factors are resonant shears `rho_a`. Such a word can be written

```text
sigma = u rho_a
```

with `u` still in the corresponding one-sided high-weight semigroup. Indeed,
move a resonant factor to the right using

```text
rho exp(D) = exp(rho D rho^(-1)) rho.
```

Conjugation by `rho` preserves the weight and local nilpotence of `D`, and the
resonant factors combine additively.

Applying the window-separation lemma to the graded algebra `rho_a(S)` gives

```text
sigma(S) intersect B_{<=6}
  subset rho_a(S) intersect B_{<=6}.
```

Hence

```text
trdeg <= 2  if a=0,
trdeg <= 1  if a!=0.
```

This closes arbitrary interleavings of the resonant family with noncommuting
one-sided high-weight source shears.

## Affine post-factors

If `L` is any affine source automorphism, then

```text
L(B_{<=6})=B_{<=6}.
```

Therefore

```text
(L sigma)(S) intersect B_{<=6}
  = L(sigma(S) intersect B_{<=6}).
```

All transcendence-degree conclusions above survive an arbitrary affine
post-factor.

## What this theorem changes

The remaining composition problem is no longer arbitrary high-degree
triangular behavior. It is confined to words that contain either

1. nonlinear factors whose weights remain inside `[-18,18]`, or
2. both positive and negative high-weight factors, so derivative-word weights
   can return to the degree-six window.

Those are the first places where genuine weight cancellation can occur.
Arbitrary wild automorphisms remain outside the theorem.
