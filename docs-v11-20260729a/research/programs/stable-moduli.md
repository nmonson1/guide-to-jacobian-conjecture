## Results

### [A Quartic Boundary-Torelli Family](../../collections/quartic-boundary-torelli.md)

Within the quartic family, stable left-right equivalence holds exactly when (rho,sigma) agrees; the normalized residual discriminant has intrinsic cusp and double-double strata and conductor divisor 2L+Q, so the family gives an affine plane of fixed-degree stable moduli.

### [All-Multiplicity Torelli for Cubic Frames](../../collections/all-multiplicity-cubic-frame-torelli.md)

For admissible cubic frames, the map remembers the complete decorated finite scheme of deleted roots—including multiplicities—and stable equivalence adds no new identifications.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Boundary Jets and Stable Moduli in the Cubic Frame](../../collections/cubic-boundary-jets-and-moduli.md)

For every coprime admissible cubic-frame pair, without assuming A squarefree, stable left-right equivalence equals ordinary left-right equivalence and is classified by scaling the decorated finite scheme Z_A=Spec C[c]/(A/c) with its unit-valued jet section B|Z_A.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [Degree Seven Minimality Status](../../collections/degree-seven-minimality-status.md)

If D_min is the minimum possible maximum ordinary total degree of a coordinate of a complex polynomial Keller counterexample in dimension three, then 4 <= D_min <= 7.

### [Equivariant Low Degree Classification](../../collections/equivariant-low-degree-classification.md)

Every equivariant Keller map of degree at most six in the analyzed family is an automorphism, so seven is the sharp threshold in that family.

### [Moduli in the Equivariant Degree-Five Family](../../collections/equivariant-degree-five-moduli.md)

### [Reciprocal Family Moduli](../../collections/reciprocal-family-moduli.md)

### [Stable Deformation Moduli](../../collections/stable-deformation-moduli.md)

For the quadratic cubic-frame slice with alpha nonzero, q=beta/alpha^2 is a complete invariant under arbitrary polynomial source-target equivalence and stabilization; the alpha=0 line is gauge-equivalent to the base map.

*Exact manuscript location*

### [The Categorical Quotient of Bounded Cubic Frames](../../collections/categorical-cubic-frame-quotient.md)

A differential-kernel model computes the invariant algebra, one root-escape wall controls global invariants, and a weighted graph closure supplies a separated replacement where the fppf orbit quotient is not algebraic.

### [Weighted-Lift Moduli Under Arbitrary Equivalence](../../collections/weighted-lift-moduli-under-arbitrary-equivalence.md)

For every generic fiber degree n at least four, the weighted-lift counterexamples contain an (n-3)-dimensional family of pairwise inequivalent maps under arbitrary polynomial left-right equivalence.
