# At a target DVR, the invariant discriminant exponent is sum f_i(e_i-1), while a monic integral primitive polynomial has exponent sum f_i(e_i-1)+2 iota_C; a reduced primitive discriminant component therefore forces one (e,f)=(2,1) ramified divisor and zero monogenic index.

## Exact statement

At a target DVR, the invariant discriminant exponent is sum f_i(e_i-1), while a monic integral primitive polynomial has exponent sum f_i(e_i-1)+2 iota_C; a reduced primitive discriminant component therefore forces one (e,f)=(2,1) ramified divisor and zero monogenic index.
