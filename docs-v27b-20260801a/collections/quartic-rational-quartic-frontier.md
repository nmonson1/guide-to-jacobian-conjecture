# Excluding Proper Rational-Quartic Leading Images

<p class="dek">Assuming the established frontier reduction to the tricuspidal type (3,(1,2)) and balanced type (2,(2,2)), no quartic Keller map in three variables has a proper rational quartic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status status-draft">Draft Public</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Assuming the established frontier reduction to the tricuspidal type (3,(1,2)) and balanced type (2,(2,2)), no quartic Keller map in three variables has a proper rational quartic as the projective image of its highest homogeneous part.
