# Fixed-Factor Conic Exclusions in Degrees Five and Six

<p class="dek">The invariant-gap method excludes the basepoint-free fixed-factor conic strata of degrees five and six, and a local normal-form argument also excludes the genuine three-variable cubic-factor quintic.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status status-draft">Draft Public</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Let \(H_D=G(A^2,AB,B^2)\), where \(A,B\) are a regular sequence and \(A/B\) is nonconstant on some component of \(G=0\). No nonautomorphic Keller map has \((D;\deg G,\deg A)=(5;1,2),(5;3,1),(6;2,2)\), or \((6;4,1)\). In the quintic \((3,1)\) case, a squarefree cubic \(G\) genuinely depending on three variables is also impossible.
