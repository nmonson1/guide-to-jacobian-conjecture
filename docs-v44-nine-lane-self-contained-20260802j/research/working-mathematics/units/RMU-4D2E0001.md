If 0=n_0<n_1<...<n_s=m are the normal indices of the Euclidean remainder sequence and d_j=n_j-n_(j-1), then the northwest rank-profile permutation of H_nu is the direct sum J_(d_1)+...+J_(d_s) of reversals of the consecutive blocks. After regular filtered row and column operations, the strict multiplication matrix is the direct sum of r^[n_(j-1)+n_j-1] I_(d_j); after restoring the common radial factor its j-th full Smith exponent is nu-m+n_(j-1)+n_j with multiplicity d_j. The southwest Krylov minor Delta_k agrees, up to the stated subresultant sign convention, with psc_(m-k)(w^nu,Q) and the corresponding rectangular Schur polynomial.
