# Intrinsic degree budgets for polynomial automorphisms

<p class="claim-tag">Lane 5 · Updated 3 August 2026</p>

Lane 5 · 2026-08-03

## Scope

Replace presentation-dependent degree bookkeeping by an invariant that can
control arbitrary polynomial source changes. Existing degree-six image-algebra
and tame-word bounds are inputs, not the desired invariant theorem.

## Setup and definitions

For a coordinate algebra generated by a Keller pair, let $\delta(Q)$ denote
the recorded semidegree/valuative budget of the second generator relative to a
chosen frame and filtration. An elementary tame move is triangular; a
separated tame word is a reduced word whose successive leading degrees cannot
return to an earlier degree face. **Frame covariance** means that the budget
and its associated graded algebra transform functorially under an allowed
change of source coordinates.

## Results to use

- The degree-six image algebra and its semidegree inequalities are exact in
  the chosen frame.
- For elementary moves and separated reduced tame words, the leading-degree
  transport and no-return conclusions are exact; the recorded degree-six
  budget permits at most two relevant steps in that class.
- The complete finite encoded enumeration covers $81$ elementary monomial
  shears: $10$ cases have rank $82$ and $71$ have rank $83$. The resonant
  family, all-exponent and lacunary tails, and separated-word checks also
  replay exactly. The failed large search was not rerun.
- These results do not cover resonant mixed-return words, wild automorphisms,
  arbitrary frame changes, or the full polynomial automorphism group.

## Example: a separated tame word

A triangular move that strictly raises the declared leading face followed by
an affine move preserving separation satisfies the no-return theorem. This is
an example of the controlled class, not evidence that every reduced tame word
can be separated.

## Live problem

Define a frame-covariant filtration or Rees object whose numerical budget
recovers $\delta$ in the known chart and yields a useful obstruction for every
polynomial source automorphism relevant to the degree-six image algebra.

## Tasks

### L5-T1 — Build the intrinsic filtration and prove covariance

Inputs: the [degree-budget packet](lane-5-source-packet.md),
the [finite enumeration receipt](lane-5-source-packet.md),
the [cubic image-algebra TeX](../proof-sources/01-cubic-incidence/main.md),
and the [relative-Jacobian filtration](../proof-sources/04-stable-moduli/appendices/all-multiplicity-relative-jacobian.md).

Deliverable: an intrinsic filtration (or a proved equivalent invariant), its
transformation law under generators of the source automorphism group, and a
theorem recovering the known elementary/separated-word bounds.

Dependencies: a declared automorphism class and proof that its generators act
on the proposed Rees object.

Limits: checking elementary generators separately is insufficient unless the
composition and resonance laws are proved.

### L5-T2 — Test the first resonant return

Inputs: L5-T1 and the exact coefficient-transport programs in the budget
packet.

Deliverable: either a bound in the first mixed-return branch with
$\delta(Q)\ge10$, or an explicit automorphism showing the proposed budget is
not invariant.

Dependencies: L5-T1.

Limits: an example in one frame must be transported back to the same intrinsic
object before it counts as a counterexample.

Alternative connections: the flatness module of Lane 1 is a welcome source of
an intrinsic filtration if the module comparison is proved.

## Limits

The current degree budgets are exact for a restricted operation class. They
do not bound arbitrary automorphisms or prove a presentation-independent
minimum degree.
