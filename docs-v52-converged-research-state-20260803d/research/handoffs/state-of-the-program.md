# Current Jacobian research portfolio

These nine independent but connected lanes are the current model-facing
research surface. Each lane defines its objects before use, separates known
results from examples and open problems, and begins at the actual frontier.
Markdown and TeX are the primary mathematical interfaces; programs appear
where they replay or construct an exact finite object.

## Connections worth testing

The most promising cross-lane links are: normalization defects with intrinsic
filtrations (1↔5), infinity gluing with chart groupoids (2↔9), escaping formal
moduli with realization complexity (3↔6), terminal quartic charts with
determinantal carriers (4↔7), and the degree-125 Newton queue with actual-chain
attachment (8↔9). These are invitations, not prerequisites: an alternate
connection is welcome when it preserves the exact hypotheses and produces the
listed deliverable.
