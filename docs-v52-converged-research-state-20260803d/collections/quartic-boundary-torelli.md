# A Quartic Boundary-Torelli Family

<p class="dek">Within the quartic family, stable left-right equivalence holds exactly when (rho,sigma) agrees; the normalized residual discriminant has intrinsic cusp and double-double strata and conductor divisor 2L+Q, so the family gives an affine plane of fixed-degree stable moduli.</p>

<span class="status status-kind">Result</span> <span class="status coverage-complete">Exact manuscript location</span>

## Precise statement

Within the quartic family, stable left-right equivalence holds exactly when (rho,sigma) agrees; the normalized residual discriminant has intrinsic cusp and double-double strata and conductor divisor 2L+Q, so the family gives an affine plane of fixed-degree stable moduli.
